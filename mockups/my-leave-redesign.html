Balance Cards
Each leave type gets its own accent colour — green (Annual), orange (Sick), red (Emergency), slate (Unpaid). Replaces the uniform sand background. Used/Pending metadata always visible without needing to tap.
+ Request Pill
The action is a labelled pill button — + Request — in the top-right header. Always reachable without scrolling, and the word "Request" makes the intent immediately clear at a glance. Compact enough to keep the header aesthetic.
Bottom Sheet Form
Form slides up from the bottom instead of expanding inline. Keeps the page behind visible. The leave type selector becomes horizontal pills showing remaining balance inline — no 2×2 grid.
Working Days Preview
Once dates are picked, a live preview of working days appears in the form before submitting — so staff know exactly what they're requesting.
Unified Request List
Pending and history are merged into one list with pill filters (All / Pending / Approved / History). Colour-coded cards make status instantly scannable.
Manager Comment
Rejected requests show the reviewer's comment inline on the card, so staff know why it was declined.
