# Workforce slice 2 (Rota): back-end decisions

Decisions taken during the back-end session (18 Oct 2026) where Paper was silent, the owner changed direction, or there were two reasonable answers. Each was built as the choice below; the last column says what changing it takes. Wire names say `organization` where the code says `site`.

| # | Question | What I chose | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R0 | **Owner change of direction (18 Oct 2026, mid-session):** is there a publish step? | **No.** No draft, no published state, no amend flow. Every set, clear, fill and copy saves at once, is live to staff, and is one audited write. The tables, enums and routes I had built for publish and amend (`RotaStatus`, `RotaPublicationKind`, `rota_publications`, `version`, publish and amend endpoints, the `rota.published` event) were removed before anything shipped; the migration was regenerated. | Owner decision. Chapter 5's "Publish and tell staff" and "Save N changes" dialogs no longer apply; the front-end session should follow this document, not those two steps. | Re-adding a publish layer means a status on `rota_weeks` and an entry staging column; not planned. |
| R1 | How are today's legacy shifts and assignments carried over? | A one-way, repeatable copy (`pnpm exec tsx src/scripts/rota-carry-over.ts`, also exported as `carryOverLegacyRota`): each legacy shift becomes a site shift template (`legacy_shift_id`), each not-yet-past assignment of a person with a clocking employee file at the same site becomes a rota entry (`legacy_assignment_id`). Overnight or malformed legacy shifts are left out. Legacy tables are only read and stay authoritative for the clock until slice 3. A week already edited in the new rota is never touched again. | The brief's recommended default. Proved on a copy: legacy tables identical before and after, second run changes nothing. | A different mapping edits `legacy-carry-over.ts`; running it for good is slice 3. |
| R2 | Where does "minimum cover" for the thin-coverage warning live? | In the Rules store (Leave group, `minimumCover` per department id), which already exists. The rota only reads it. | One place for the number; Chapter 13 already draws it there. | None needed. |
| R3 | When does a change need a reason? | A change to a shift **today or tomorrow** (Nairobi days) needs 3 to 200 characters; otherwise it is optional. The reason is stored in the audit entry. Past days: 409 `PAST_DAY_READ_ONLY`. Applies to set, clear, fill, pattern, copy and a shift-time change that moves a shift on those days. | Owner decision. | `REASON_DAYS_AHEAD` in `rota.constants.ts`. |
| R4 | Leave override. | A shift on an approved-leave day is allowed only with an `overrideReason` (409 `LEAVE_OVERRIDE_REQUIRED` otherwise); the reason is kept on the entry and in the audit entry. Leave reads nothing until slice 5 (a registered lookup returns none). | Chapter 5 step 4; slice 5 plugs into `registerLeaveLookup`. | Slice 5 registers the real lookup. |
| R5 | Copy ("start from last week") onto a week that already has shifts. | It fills **empty** cells only; occupied cells are kept and counted ("kept"). Past days are skipped. Clashes (leave, sent to another site that day, not employed, shift no longer available) are listed in the preview and never copied. A copy that would write nothing answers 409 `NOTHING_TO_COPY`. | Overwriting someone's planned week silently would be worse than skipping. | Change `planCopy`. |
| R6 | Change notices: how are they batched? | Each person has a timer. Every change restarts it; after **5 minutes without a further change** one `rota.shifts_changed` event goes out for that person (dates, count, who changed it). The rules store has no field for it, so 5 minutes is a constant (`CHANGE_QUIET_MS`). The batcher is in memory: a server restart loses a notice that was still waiting; the shifts themselves are saved. Delivery only logs until Notifications exist. | Owner decision (5 minutes). In-memory is the smallest thing that works with no queue; a durable outbox belongs with the Notifications slice. | Move the constant to the Rules store, or back the batcher with a table when Notifications arrive. |
| R7 | A cross-site write. | A manager editing a person at another site gets **404** (the person is not found for them), not 403. Reads are 404 too. A role without the capability, and an in-site person outside a head's department, get **403**. | A write must not reveal that a person exists at another site. Same rule as the Organisation endpoints. | Throw `ForbiddenError` in `cells-apply.ts`. |
| R8 | Which people can be on a rota? | People whose file says they clock in (`tracksTime`), employed on the day, at the rota's site. A person on loan elsewhere is a copy clash. People who do not clock in (Branch Manager, HR, Director) get 400 `EMPLOYEE_NOT_ON_ROTA`. | Brief: "a rota entry only for people who tracks time". | n/a |
| R9 | Cost on the grid. | Only for a caller with company-wide pay read (HR, Director). A monthly salary counts its weekly share (12 / 52) for anyone with a shift that week; a daily rate counts the rate for each planned day; cents, never floats; labelled as an estimate. Absent (not zero) for everyone else, and never on the printed rota or in audit entries. | Proposal 5.1: "cost only for roles that may see pay". A Branch Manager and a head never read pay. | `week-cost.ts` |
| R10 | Shift templates: edit or version? | Name, letter, colour and departments change in place. A change to the times or the break moves **upcoming** shifts (today on) to the new times and tells each person; if the shift also has days in the past, the new times are a **new template** and the old one is retired with `superseded_by_id`, so past rotas keep what was worked. Retire is refused while upcoming shifts use it (409 `TEMPLATE_IN_USE`). | Chapter 5 step 6: "published shifts keep what staff were told". Never edit history. | `templates-update-service.ts` |
| R11 | Who may write templates? | New capability `rota.template_write`: Branch Manager and Store Manager for their own site's shifts, HR, Director and System Admin everywhere (company-wide shifts need scope `all`). Logged in `slice-0-contract.md` §12. | Chapter 5 step 6 footnote: "HR and the Director edit company-wide shifts; a Branch Manager can add shifts for their own site". | Edit the grants in `workforce-access.ts` and its matrix test. |
| R12 | Department scope on the grid and writes. | A department head reads and edits only the departments they head (their head rows), via `rota.read` / `rota.write` at scope `dept`. A staff member (scope `own`) has no grid and reads only their own shifts (`GET /workforce/rota/mine`). The Accountant has an own-shifts view only; HR builds the Accountant's rota (decision D18). | Access table. | n/a |
| R13 | Weeks and limits. | A week must start on the Rules "week starts on" day (Monday by default). A rota may be planned up to 26 weeks ahead and read up to 104 weeks back; up to 3 shifts a day per person (a second shift is a double-shift warning). Letters are unique among the shifts one site can see. | Keeps the grid and the audit entries bounded. | `rota.constants.ts` |
| R14 | Audit shape. | One entry per request (never per cell), category TIME: counts plus up to 100 `{ person, date, from, to }` lines using shift names. Never a pay figure. The carry-over writes one SYSTEM entry per company only when it changed something. | One entry per bulk action (slice 0 rule). | n/a |
| R15 | Standing pattern vs the removed "brush" button. | Both are API features: `fill` (one shift or a day off across people and dates) and `pattern` (one shift per weekday for chosen people). The screen decides which buttons to draw (Chapter 5 removed the brush button; the drag still exists). | The API costs nothing extra and lets the front end choose. | Delete the route. |
