# Workforce Segment 2B (Payroll back end, part 2): decisions

Written 20 Oct 2026 on branch `feat/workforce-2b-payroll-backend` (cut from `feat/workforce-2a-payroll-backend`, not pushed). Each row is a question the spec or the brief left open (or a place the build departs from the spec), what was built, why, and what changing it takes. Code: `backend/src/modules/workforce/payroll/` (`publish/`, `corrections/`, `bank/`, `paid/`, `exports/`). Part 1's decisions: [decisions-2a-payroll-backend.md](decisions-2a-payroll-backend.md).

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| X1 | Payslip status after publish. The brief says PUBLISHED and LOCKED payslip values. | The payslip enum is unchanged (`READY`). Status is **derived** from the month: `READY` before publish, `PUBLISHED` from publish on (a paid month shows the same word, with `locked: true` on the list). | Payslip rows are insert-only (a database trigger), so a status could never be updated. A derived word cannot disagree with the month. | Add enum values and a status table beside the payslip, written at publish and paid. |
| X2 | Where does `PS-nnnn` live? | In `payroll_publications` (month, person, number from the sequence `payroll_payslip_seq`), not on the payslip row. A correction keeps the number. | The payslip row is immutable and the number is issued later. Unique `(month, person)` makes a double publish impossible; the sequence allows gaps. | Move the number onto the payslip insert and reissue it per version. |
| X3 | Exports above 100 people (background job and a notification). | Synchronous up to 400 people (413 above), 30 s timeout, 12 per person per 10 minutes. **No background job.** | 1A's exports have the same shape. Wendo has about 90 people. A job needs the queue and a file store that do not exist for this. | A BullMQ job returning `202 {jobId}` and `GET exports/jobs/:id`. |
| X4 | "Never any data from outside the snapshot." | The snapshot at approval now also holds each person's filing numbers (KRA PIN, NSSF, SHA), the lines' `base`, `baseAmount`, `rate`, the pay-out method and the people left out. Exports of an approved month read only the latest snapshot revision. A snapshot written by part 1 lacks these, so those columns are blank for it. | The statutory schedules need the numbers; reading the live record would let a file change after approval. | Nothing for new months. |
| X5 | Corrections and snapshot revisions. | Each correction adds snapshot revision r+1 in the same transaction (copy of r with the one payslip replaced, totals summed again, the correction listed). The approval signature, date and document number stay those of revision 1. | E8: documents of an approved month never change, and say `Revision n`. | The revision builder is `corrections/snapshot-revision.ts`. |
| X6 | What can a correction change. | Five kinds (P21): late days counted (down only, latest dropped first), unpaid absence days (0.5 steps, up to the month length), authorised overtime minutes per day type, basic for the month (monthly-salary people only), an extra line (add up to 5, remove by id). The engine recomputes the one payslip from the stored input with the month's frozen rules. | Late days cannot rise because the timesheet is frozen; daily-rate pay has no "monthly basic". | `corrections/apply-correction.ts` and the validators. |
| X7 | "Preparer rule" on corrections. | The only separation is `SELF_CORRECTION`: nobody corrects their own payslip. The preparer rule (P20) is about approval and is not applied. | E9 and S11 name only the own-payslip rule. The Accountant who approved may correct. | One check in `corrections/correction-prepare.ts`. |
| X8 | When is the bank file voided. | When a correction changes the net of a **bank-paid** person (a net-neutral correction does not), **and** when a download no longer matches the month now (a changed account, bank or amount). The second case is found by comparing the file's hash with a fresh build, so no hook into People is needed. The pay-out card shows such a file as voided at once; the next download (or a refused Mark as paid) writes the void. | A hook into People would couple two modules. A hash compare cannot miss a change. | Replace the hash compare with a People event. |
| X9 | Re-downloading after Paid. | Allowed for the Accountant, audited with `again: true`, writes no row and changes nothing. | S12 keeps "Download again". The month is locked, so a new bank-file row would be refused by the database. | None. |
| X10 | Mark as paid on a paid month. | `409 ALREADY_PAID` ("Someone marked this month paid a moment ago.") rather than `MONTH_LOCKED`; every other write answers `MONTH_LOCKED`. | S8 words that case separately. | One line in `paid/paid-service.ts`. |
| X11 | Who blocks Mark as paid. | A bank-paid person with a net above 0 and no complete account (bank name, account name, account number) blocks it (`PEOPLE_CANNOT_BE_PAID`, listed in the pay-out card). A net of 0 is listed apart and does not block. People paid by mobile money, cash or not set are counted apart and do not block (not in the file). Left-out people have no payslip. Checked live, so fixing the account unblocks it. | S8, P23. | `bank/bank-rows.ts`. |
| X12 | The lock in the database. | A trigger `payroll_locked_month_guard` refuses INSERT, UPDATE and DELETE on the month, its sites, runs, payslips, lines, review lines, adjustments, left-out rows, snapshots, bank files and payslip numbers once `locked_at` is set (message starts `MONTH_LOCKED`). Checks tie `state = PAID` to `locked_at`, `paid_by`, `paid_at`; `PUBLISHED` needs the publisher. The dev teardown door (`wendo.allow_payroll_edit`) also opens this guard. `payroll_exports` and idempotency keys are not guarded (an export after Paid is allowed). | A bug in a service must not be able to change a paid month. | The migration `..._part2_lock`. |
| X13 | Reads must follow corrections. | Every read that sums or lists payslips (list, totals, tax tab, estimate, last month's comparison) takes the latest version per person (`currentPayslipIds`). Once published, the month totals are summed from those; the run's stored totals never change. | A correction adds a row; counting both would double a person. | `reads/reads-repository.ts`, `reads/current-totals.ts`. |
| X14 | Bank file format. | `BankFileFormat` interface and a registry; Generic CSV (UTF-8, CRLF, header row, the nine columns of E7, no byte-order mark, cells starting `= + - @` neutralised against spreadsheet formulas). The active format is a constant `ACTIVE_BANK_FORMAT`, not a company setting (no settings store exists). | Same as E7 without a store. | A setting read in `bank/bank-build.ts`. |
| X15 | Bank file never stored. | Rebuilt from the current payslips and the live bank details; the row keeps version, name, hash, count, total, who and when. The download answers with `X-Bank-File-Hash`; Mark as paid takes that hash. | E7. A stored file would be another copy of account numbers. | None. |
| X16 | Zip without a dependency. | `exports/render/zip.ts` writes a stored (uncompressed) zip with Node's `zlib.crc32`. | PDFs are already compressed; nothing in the project zips. | Replace with a zip library. |
| X17 | Register PDF with many tax tables. | One column per table up to 7; more fold into "Other statutory" (type is never shrunk). The Excel keeps every column. Figures band has six cells; the CASUAL and PETTY CASH cells of the spec text are not drawn while the Casual feature does not exist. | E8 forbids smaller type; the spec lists eight names for "six cells". | `exports/render/register-layout.ts`. |
| X18 | Payslip PDF. | Segment 3 owns the A4 payslip; the zip needs one, so a plain A4 version is drawn here from the snapshot (header, employee, earnings, deductions with workings, net, employer block, approver's signature, QR, CORRECTED and "replaces version n" for version 2). Staff phone endpoints and the P9 stay Segment 3's. | The zip must contain something real. | Segment 3 replaces `exports/render/payslip-pdf.ts`. |
| X19 | Audit of money. | `payroll.payslip_corrected` carries old and new gross, net, deductions and the reason (E9); `bank_file_downloaded` carries count, total and hash. No PIN and no account number appears anywhere. New actions: `published`, `payslip_corrected`, `bank_file_downloaded`, `bank_file_voided`, `marked_paid`, `exported` (corrections, voids and exports may be filed under either payroll category, by role). | E9. Part 1's "no figure in audit" rule was for the entries that had none. | `_shared/audit/audit-actions.ts`. |
| X20 | Notifications. | Told after the commit, best effort: staff on publish, correction and paid; the Accountants on publish and when a correction voids a file; HR on paid. Wording keys `payroll.payslip_published`, `payroll.payslip_corrected`, `payroll.bank_file_voided`, `payroll.month_paid`; no figures. Events `payroll.month_published`, `payroll.payslip_corrected`, `payroll.month_paid` are emitted for Segment 3 and 1A. | E9, E11. | The wording table (front end). |
| X21 | Blocked publish "a person Left out". | Not blocked. Left-out people are paid next month (P15) and have no payslip. | A blocked publish would stop every month with one left-out person. | None. |
| X22 | Capabilities. | None added: `payroll.publish`, `pay`, `correct`, `export` already existed from part 1 and match E9. Recorded in `slice-0-contract.md` §12. The front-end list `capabilities.ts` still says `payrun.*` and must change to `payroll.*`. | One access table. | The front end. |
| X23 | Idempotency and concurrency. | Every POST takes an idempotency key (the same key answers the first result, another action or month with the same key is `409 IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED`); the month row is locked `FOR UPDATE` inside every transaction; `stateVersion` on month writes; `expectedVersion` on a correction; bank downloads are keyed per person. Proved with two simultaneous calls for publish, correction and Mark as paid. | E9. | None. |
| X24 | Found by the HTTP proof. | HR's correction failed to file `bank_file_voided` under HR's category (`AUDIT_CATEGORY_NOT_ALLOWED`); fixed by allowing both categories. The database flow had the Accountant correcting, so only real logins found it; the flow now has HR correct first. | Real roles matter. | Done. |
