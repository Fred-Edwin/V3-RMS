## Menu proposal (finding 41, for the owner; to be drawn in Paper before any code)

1. **Organise by feature, not by role.** One list of modules, the same names for every role; each role sees only the modules it may use. Today the groups are named per role (`dir-branches`, `branches`, `central-store`), which is why the same thing sits in different places.
2. **Top level is the module; sub-links sit underneath.** For example: Home; People (Employees, Organisation); Time (Today, Shifts, Timesheets, Leave, Casual pay); Pay (Payroll, Payslips); Operations (Branch day, Waste, Incidents, Discounts, Other income); Inventory (Stock and counts, Requisitions, Prep, Catalog, Suppliers, Purchasing, Receiving, Carriers); Reports (Dashboard, Analytics, Audit log); Company settings (Rules, Branch settings). Not more than 8 modules for any role.
3. **Only the active module is open.** Others are collapsed to one line. Sub-links use the approved curved connector rail. A collapsed module shows the sum of its badges, so nothing waiting is hidden.
4. **Traceable.** The breadcrumb always reads Module / Page, and the active link and its module are both highlighted, so a person always knows where they are and how they got there.
5. **Personal items out of the list.** My file, My payslips and Profile live in the account block at the bottom; they are not a "Me" group.
6. **A jump box.** A search at the top of the menu (and Ctrl+K) that finds any page or person by name, so a long menu is never the only way in.
7. **Old links retire.** The old HR group goes as its pages are rebuilt (finding 9); no Inbox in Workforce (finding 39). Phones keep the same list in the drawer.
8. **How it is built.** The navigation table already holds every link; each row gets a `module` and a `parent` instead of a per-role group, and the shell renders the accordion. Rows stay the single source; `nav-table.test.ts` still checks them against the route guard.

## Navigation naming convention (finding 74, proposed; owner to approve)
1. **Home is always called "Home".** It is the first link for every role and opens that role's Workforce Home (finding 73). "Dashboard" is not used for a landing page. A page of charts and live figures is called by what it shows ("Sales today", "Analytics"), never "Dashboard".
2. **One word for one thing, everywhere.** People are "Employees" (the list) and "Organisation" (departments and heads); the head's team is "My team"; time is "Timesheets"; leave is "Leave"; pay is "Payroll" (for the Accountant) and "My payslips" (for the person). the schedule is "Shifts" (owner's choice over "Rota", 21 Oct), also at department level, never "Department Shifts".
3. **Names are short nouns in sentence case** ("Stock and counts", not "Stock & counts"; "Audit log"), one to three words, with no "Manage", "View" or "My" unless the page is the person's own (My file, My payslips, My leave).
4. **Personal pages start with "My"** and sit in the account block at the bottom (finding 41, point 5); every other link is a work page.
5. **The group name is the feature**, the same for every role (People, Time, Pay, Operations, Inventory, Reports, Company settings); a link never repeats its group's name.
6. **A food "Menu" is "Menu items"** so it never clashes with the navigation menu.
