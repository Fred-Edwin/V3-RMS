# Segment 2: Payroll, engine, documents, permissions and rollout

19 October 2026. Companion to [2-payroll-spec.md](2-payroll-spec.md) (screens S0 to S12). Choices are logged in [decisions-2-spec.md](decisions-2-spec.md) (P1, P2 ...). **This file states structure, not tax law.** No rate, band, threshold or relief is written here as fact: they come from the Accountant's confirmed Statutory tables. Paper's figures are illustrative.

## E1. The month status machine

A payroll month is one calendar month for the whole company. It sits beside the per-site timesheet months of 1A and reads them.

| State | Meaning | Who moves it | Allows |
|---|---|---|---|
| OPEN | The month has not ended. Estimate only (S10). | Nobody (derived from the date) | Read the estimate. No export, no payslips. |
| WAITING | Month ended; at least one **included** site has not approved its timesheets, or the tax tables are not confirmed. | Derived | Remind a site. Director approves for a site late (P4). |
| IN_REVIEW | Every included site approved and tables confirmed; the system calculated (run n). | **The system**, automatically within 15 minutes of the last approval (a job), or HR's `Calculate again` | HR ticks lines, adds adjustments, leaves people out, recalculates. Draft exports. |
| TO_APPROVE | HR sent it. | HR (`payroll.review`), `Send to the Accountant` | Accountant approves or sends back. HR can no longer change lines. |
| TO_PUBLISH | Accountant approved with PIN. Document `PAY-YYYY-MM` issued, snapshot frozen. | Accountant (`payroll.approve`) | HR publishes. Exports are final (signed). No recalculation. |
| PUBLISHED | HR published; staff see their payslip. Called "To pay" in the list. | HR (`payroll.publish`) | **Corrections** (S11). Bank file. Accountant marks paid. |
| PAID | Accountant marked it paid. Locked. | Accountant (`payroll.pay`) | Read, export, view. Every write answers `409 MONTH_LOCKED`. |

Rules: forward only, except **Send back to HR** (TO_APPROVE to IN_REVIEW, Accountant, reason). There is no reopen of a Paid month and no Director reopen (owner). Every transition carries the month's `stateVersion` (integer, bumped on each change) and an idempotency key; a stale version answers `409 CHANGED_UNDERNEATH`. The approver is never the user who sent, recalculated or ticked (`SELF_APPROVAL`). How it extends 1A: 1A's OPEN, TO_APPROVE, APPROVED are per site; Payroll's WAITING is "some included site is not APPROVED". The 1A month row says `Paid` once its payroll month is PAID (a read of `payroll.month_paid`), otherwise `Approved`. The 1A lock (`PERIOD_APPROVED`) stays; Payroll adds `MONTH_LOCKED`. Calculation **never starts** before every included site is approved: it is a rule, not a convention.

## E2. Calculation inputs (per person, per month)

1. **Who is included:** employees of included sites (E10) with an active employment spell that overlaps the month, not left out by HR.
2. **Hours:** `getApprovedHours(userId, {start, end})` gives `workedMinutes` and `overtimeApprovedMinutes` (minutes only, approved months only). A site-month's frozen snapshot (1A) supplies late minutes, late days, absent days and leave days per person; payroll reads the snapshot, never raw clock events.
3. **Pay profile:** `payAmountOn(employeeId, date)` for the amount in force (a month with a pay change mid-month splits by calendar days at each change); `PayProfile` gives pay basis (monthly amount), `overtimeEligible`, standard week hours (default divisor from Rules), payout method and bank details.
4. **Rules** via `getEffectiveRules(siteId, monthLastDay)` frozen into `rulesUsed`: Overtime (multipliers, confirmed), Lateness, Leave, Holidays (`payMultiplier`, confirmed), Statutory tables (confirmed), Casual work (memo only), Week and breaks. A rule not confirmed (`isPayConfirmed` false) stops the calculation (the month stays WAITING with the banner).
5. **Adjustments** HR added in review (S5).
6. **Casual pay** as a memo from the Casual feature's door (0 until it exists).
7. **Leave days** counted from approved leave; **0 until the Leave feature is built** (the Leave card and its box show `0`; leave never reduces pay by Rules).

## E3. Confirmation gating

Payroll refuses to calculate unless `isPayConfirmed(month)` is true for Statutory, the Overtime multipliers and, if a holiday fell in the month, the Holidays pay rate. The message names the missing item and the role: `Waiting for the Accountant to confirm the tax tables.` A version confirmed after calculation never changes a calculated month (the month keeps its frozen `rulesUsed`). The Statutory tables **ship empty**, so on day one every month is WAITING for the tables; the Accountant types and confirms them in Rules, Statutory (built).

## E4. The calculation engine (generic and table-driven)

A pure function `calculatePayslip(input, rules) → lines[]`, in `payroll/engine/`, with no database access. **No tax rate, band, threshold, relief or contribution name appears in code**; statutory tables are data of kinds `BANDS` (progressive), `RATE` (flat percentage with optional floor and ceiling) and `OTHER`, each with a `base` (`GROSS`, `BASIC`, `TAXABLE`, `BASIC_PLUS_ALLOWANCES`) and an `employeeShare` and optional `employerShare`. Order of calculation:

1. **Basic for the month.** Monthly amount; prorated by calendar days for a joiner, a leaver or a pay change (`amount × days in effect ÷ days in month`). Hourly or daily staff: the profile basis × approved worked minutes ÷ 60.
2. **Earnings.** Overtime pay = `authorised overtime minutes ÷ 60 × hourly rate × multiplier`, where hourly rate = basic ÷ `standardWeekHours × 52 ÷ 12` (Paper's `Basic ÷ 208` is the 48-hour-week form; the divisor shown is whatever the profile and Rules produce) and the multiplier is the confirmed ordinary, rest-day or holiday one by the day's type (a day's type from the Holidays list and the rota rest days); only for `overtimeEligible` staff and only for **authorised** minutes (never recorded minutes). Holiday pay = hours worked on a public holiday × hourly rate × the Holidays `payMultiplier`. Adjustments of kind allowance, incentive and arrears are added; each carries its own `taxable` flag.
3. **Pay deductions before gross is fixed.** Unpaid absence = `unpaid absence days × basic ÷ divisor` (Paper: ÷ 30; a Leave-group field `unpaidDayDivisor`, P11; approved leave never reduces pay). Lateness = per the Lateness rule: `record only` deducts nothing; `after N late days` deducts from the (N)th counted late day on, at `late minutes ÷ 60 × hourly rate` for the minutes counted from that day; `from the first minute` counts all; the total is capped at `deductionCapPercentOfPay × basic ÷ 100` (P12; the rule has no flat amount, see inconsistency 11). **Gross pay** = basic + overtime + holiday pay + taxable and non-taxable allowances − unpaid absence − lateness.
4. **Statutory deductions**, in the table order the Accountant set, each computed on its `base` from the gross (or its base): `BANDS` apply each band's width and rate in turn; `RATE` applies the percentage, then the floor and ceiling if present; the **employee share** is taken from pay, the **employer share** is a cost only (not on the net).
5. **PAYE** is a `BANDS` table whose base is `TAXABLE` (gross minus the deductions the Accountant marked as allowable, such as pension-type contributions, minus non-taxable lines); **personal relief** is a monthly amount on the table, subtracted from the computed tax, never below zero (`max(0, tax − relief)`). Which other tables reduce the taxable base is a flag on each table (`reducesTaxable`), set by the Accountant. The engine never decides that a table is "NSSF" or "PAYE": it reads `kind`, `base`, `reducesTaxable`, `isIncomeTax`.
6. **Other deductions:** adjustments of kind advance repaid, HELB and other deduction.
7. **Net** = gross − statutory employee shares − PAYE − other deductions. **Total cost** (KPI) = gross + employer shares + casual memo.
8. **Casual pay** is a memo, never in any payslip, register row, schedule or bank file.

**What a payslip line records (each line shows its working).** Every line stores `code`, `label`, `kind` (EARNING, DEDUCTION, STATUTORY, MEMO), `amount`, `taxable`, `base`, `baseAmount`, `rate` or `bandsUsed`, `quantity` and `unit`, `source` (RULE with `ruleKey` and `version`, PROFILE, TIMESHEET with the site-month document number, ADJUSTMENT with the id and reason), and `workingText` built from these (for example `28,303 × 6% = 1,698` or `4 late days, 52 min; deducted from the third day on`). The phone and the A4 payslip show `workingText` verbatim. Employer shares are stored as MEMO lines.

**First month.** The Accountant checks the first month against a hand calculation: S3's `Check one person by hand` drawer prints the lines and workings, S6 asks the Accountant to confirm at least 3 payslips were checked, and the audit records it (P16). The engine is not a tax authority: the spec states structure only, and the Statutory tables are the Accountant's responsibility.

## E5. Rounding and sanity checks

- **Rounding.** Work in decimal strings with 4 decimal places inside a line; round each **line** half up to 2 decimals when stored; gross, deductions and net are sums of stored (2-decimal) lines, so a payslip always adds up. Screens show whole shillings (display rounding half up; Excel and the bank file show 2 decimals). Totals are sums of stored lines, never of rounded displays. PAYE and each statutory line round **per person**, never on the total. A minute is never rounded; hours are minutes ÷ 60.
- **Blocks** (the month cannot be sent): negative net; a person with hours or employment but no pay profile (unless left out); a bank-paid person without a bank account; statutory not confirmed; a person moved between systems (E10); a total of employee deductions above gross.
- **Warns** (HR ticks): net changed by more than 25% from last month (`payroll-constants.ts`); overtime above 50% of basic; lateness cap reached; missing KRA PIN, NSSF or SHA number; duplicate bank account; bank details changed since last month; employed with no timesheet days; first payroll month for a person (`NEW_JOINER`); a person left out earlier (`ARREARS`).
- **Whole-month checks** (blocks): sum of payslips equals the by-site table equals the register total equals the bank file total plus left-out; if a check fails the run is stored as FAILED and HR sees `The totals do not agree. Calculate again. If it happens again, tell the developer.`

## E6. Corrections until Paid, and the lock

One payslip at a time (S11). A correction is a **new payslip version** (insert-only rows, never updates): `version n+1` with the full lines, a `diff` against the previous version, `reason`, `correctedById`, `correctedAt`. The engine recomputes that one person with the month's **frozen** rule versions and the same snapshot hours, plus the changed inputs (late days, unpaid absence days, authorised overtime minutes, basic for the month, an adjustment line). Month totals recompute from the current versions. Order of effects in one transaction: insert version, recompute totals, void any downloaded bank file (E7), audit, notify the staff member (no figures) and the Accountant if a file was voided, emit `payroll.payslip_corrected` (1A reads it), bump `stateVersion`. A correction needs `payroll.correct` (HR or the Accountant), a reason (5 to 280 characters), the corrector's PIN, never on the corrector's own payslip. Allowed only in PUBLISHED. In TO_APPROVE or TO_PUBLISH HR cannot correct a line (the Accountant sends back instead). **The lock:** `markPaid` sets `lockedAt`; from then every payroll write (correction, adjustment, recalculation, publish, bank file creation) answers `409 MONTH_LOCKED` "This month is paid and locked." A later change is a line in next month's payroll, or the developer's out-of-band correction with its own audit entry. Staff do not raise anything in the system (owner): they phone HR or the Accountant.

## E7. The bank file and the estimator

**Bank file (bank only, owner).** Generic **CSV**, UTF-8, comma separated, header row, one row per bank-paid person with net above 0, columns in this order as Paper's pay-out card implies: `Reference` (the payslip number `PS-nnnn`), `Account name`, `Bank name`, `Branch`, `Account number`, `Amount` (KES, 2 decimals, dot), `Currency` (`KES`), `Narration` (`Wendo salary Sep 2026`), `Employee number`. A total is not a row. **The file format is a business choice and is replaceable per bank:** the builder is `bank-file/formats/generic-csv.ts` behind a `BankFileFormat` interface (`id`, `columns`, `render(rows)`), a company setting names the active format (Generic CSV by default); a bank's own layout is a new format file, no screen changes. File name `bank-2026-09.csv`, a correction creates `bank-2026-09-v2.csv`. Each download writes a `pay_bank_files` row (version, hash, count, total, downloadedBy, downloadedAt, state) and an audit entry. **Voided** when any payslip's net changes or a person's payment details change after a download: the row's state becomes VOIDED with the reason; the Accountant needs a new version to mark paid. Account numbers are masked everywhere except in the file; the file is never stored (rebuilt from payslips) and never emailed.

**The estimator** (S10, S1): `payroll/estimate/estimate.ts`, pure, run nightly at 00:30 Nairobi for the open month and on demand for WAITING months. Inputs: the same pay profiles and spells as E2, authorised overtime so far, unpaid absence to date, last month's calculated gross. Output per site as S10 describes. It uses no approved timesheets, no statutory tables and no payslip rows, so it works on the first day and on a site not yet approved. It is labelled an estimate on screen, never exported, never fed into a document.

More than one page is not allowed: more than 8 tables trigger a smaller second block, never smaller type.

Paper draws the Excel application, so values come from the picture by eye (header fill light blue-grey, total row bold with a thick top rule).

- **All payslips.** Segment 3's A4 payslip, one PDF each, in a zip; a month above 100 people is a background job (a notification arrives).

A correction creates **revision r+1** of the snapshot (the earlier one is kept): the register, summary and statutory exports then say `Revision 2` and re-sign nothing (the Accountant's signature stays on revision 1, the footer says `Revision 2 reflects 1 correction after approval`). **Refusal rule.** A role without `payroll.read` answers **403** (the page exists but is not theirs; same as Statutory); a month key that does not exist answers **404**; a payslip of a person outside the caller's reach answers **404** (staff reading another person's payslip), never 403. A role that may read but not act answers 403 with the reason code. HR and the Accountant are both able to correct, but never their own payslip (`SELF_CORRECTION` 403). The preparer cannot approve (`SELF_APPROVAL`). HR cannot approve a site's timesheets (1A D7). Director and System Admin never get a write button.

**Told** (Notifications row and menu badge; wording in `notification-copy.ts`; **never a pay figure**): HR: `Nyeri Town approved September 2026.` (1A), `The Accountant sent September back: {reason}` (reasons contain no figures by rule: a free-text reason is shown only to payroll roles). The Accountant: `September is ready to approve.` (on send). HR: `September was approved. Publish the payslips.` Staff on publish: `Your September payslip is ready.` (push and Inbox; opens My payslips behind the payslip code). Staff on correction: `Your September payslip was corrected. See version 2.` The Accountant on a correction after a bank file download: `A payslip was corrected after the bank file was downloaded. Download a new file.` Staff and HR on paid: `September pay has been sent.` A site's approver on a reminder: `Payroll is waiting for your September timesheets.` The Director, HR: a site waiting more than 5 days after month end (reminder only, Segment 6). Menu badges: HR on Payroll counts IN_REVIEW open lines and TO_PUBLISH; the Accountant counts TO_APPROVE and PUBLISHED-to-pay; the Home "Needs you" card rows open Payroll on the right tab (Segment 6).

Audit entries for money carry amounts and are readable only by `audit.read.payroll` (HR, Accountant, Director, System Admin); the System Admin's security alerts never contain amounts. The Audit log's Area filter "Workforce" shows them.

**Concurrency and idempotency.** `stateVersion` on every month write; `expectedVersion` on every payslip write; an idempotency key on every POST (same key answers the first result, a different key on a finished step answers 409). A row lock on the month for every transition (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`). A double click, two HR users and a flaky connection all end in one result.

## E10. Rollout: the old payslips and the new Payroll

**The problem.** The legacy payslip code (`payslip-routes`, `payslip-service`, `payslip-repository`, the `payslips` table with `@@unique([siteId, userId, payPeriod])`) still pays everyone. The new attendance clock is switched on **per site** (`AttendanceSiteSwitch`; pilot Nyeri Town). A site that is off has no approved timesheets.

**Decision (P1).**
1. **A Payroll month includes only the sites whose attendance switch was on for the whole month and that approved their timesheets.** (A site switched on mid-month starts with the first full month after, which is also when its first timesheet month exists, 1A.) Sites that are not included stay on the old payslip run for that month.
2. **Nobody is paid twice, by one rule in one place:** a **person is covered by exactly one system per month**, decided by their **site**. The new payroll month stores its `includedSiteIds`. The old payslip code gets a small, additive guard (below): it refuses to save, publish or revert a payslip for a site-and-period that the new Payroll covers, and the old payroll screens grey out those people. The legacy tables stay untouched (no column, no migration on `payslips`).
3. **A person who moves site** during the month (a transfer, Segment People) is paid by the system of the site where they spent most days; a flag `MOVED_BETWEEN_SYSTEMS` (blocking review line) appears if the person has approved hours in an included site **and** a legacy payslip draft exists in the same period at another site, so HR resolves it by hand. Loans (staff sent to another site) are paid by their home site's system.
4. **The old run keeps working for the rest:** sites not included keep creating, publishing and reverting legacy payslips as today, with no change in their screens.
5. **The month page for a site not yet on the new system** (S1 and S10): the site appears in the sites table as `Not on the new system yet · Paid by the old payslips`, is not counted in "3 of 4", is not in the estimate or any total, and a quiet line under the table says `{n} people at {Site} are still paid by the old payslips.`

**The minimal guard in the old code (additive, small).** A new function in `workforce/payroll/` exported through the workforce index, `isSitePeriodOnNewPayroll(siteId, payPeriod)`: true when a payroll month with that `monthKey` exists whose `includedSiteIds` contains the site **and its state is at least IN_REVIEW** (the month's inclusion is fixed the moment it is first calculated; before that the site is still free for the old run only if its switch is off: if the switch is on and the month is not yet calculated the old run is also refused, so that no one starts a legacy payslip for a site the new system will pay). The guard is: `payslipService.bulkUpsert`, `publish` and `revert` call it first and throw `ConflictError('This site and month are paid in the new Payroll.', 'PAID_IN_NEW_PAYROLL')`; `payslipService.list` and `getById` are untouched (history stays readable). It is one import and four lines in `payslip-service.ts`, with its own test, and **no schema or data change to the legacy tables**. The legacy `bulkUpsert` already skips locked rows; nothing there changes.

**Switch rule.** `attendanceSwitch` on for a site on the 1st of month M means the site is in Payroll from the first month **after** its first approved timesheet month ends; a site is never in Payroll without an approved timesheet month, and once a site has been in Payroll for a month it is never again paid by the old run (a switch turned off later does not return it: the guard also refuses the old run for any site that has appeared in any payroll month, `siteEverOnNewPayroll`). Turning a switch off is itself blocked while a payroll month for that site is not PAID.

**Pilot and cutover.** Nyeri Town first (owner's pilot): the first Payroll month contains Nyeri Town only; the Accountant checks it by hand (E4); the old run continues for the other sites; later sites join month by month as their timesheets are approved. No big bang, no migration of old payslips: past months stay in the old tables and are shown in the months list from the legacy `payslips` summary as **read-only history rows** (`Aug 2026 · Paid · Old payslips`) only when the owner asks (not built in Segment 2: the list shows new-system months only, P33).

**The first-month ritual.** Month 1 of the first site: the Accountant hand-checks at least 3 payslips (S6 checkbox), HR checks every blocking line, and the old run for that site's people is **not** run. The owner and the Accountant decide the first month's date; nothing in the build does it automatically other than the switch rule.

## E11. What the desktop hands to Segment 3 (staff phone, A4 payslip, casual pay sheet)

- **A payslip view model** (`GET .../payslips/:id` for payroll readers, `GET /workforce/me/payslips/:id` behind the payslip code for staff): header (company, employee name, employee number, KRA PIN, NSSF and SHA numbers masked, pay method bank masked), lines (earnings, deductions) each with `label`, `amount`, `workingText`, `year to date`, PAYE after personal relief shown as one line, employer contributions block (from MEMO lines), leave balance (0 until Leave), `version`, `replacesVersion`, `documentNumber PS-nnnn`, `publishedAt`, signer and QR payload. Amounts hidden by default is a client behaviour (Segment 3); the API returns amounts.
- **Year to date** per line (sum of the person's current payslip versions from January to this month) and **P9 data** (per month: gross, taxable, PAYE, relief) computed by the engine's year query; the document is not drawn.
- **Events** `payroll.month_published`, `payroll.payslip_corrected`, `payroll.month_paid` for the notifications and the staff inbox.
- **Casual pay sheet** `CPS-nnnn` and approved-sheet totals by day and department for S4 (`getApprovedCasualDays(month)` door from the Casual feature); until it exists the tab says so (S4).
- **My payslips staff list** reads only PUBLISHED or PAID versions for the staff member, newest version per month.
