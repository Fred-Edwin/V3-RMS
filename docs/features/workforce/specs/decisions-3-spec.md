# Segment 3 (Payslips and casual pay on the phone, and the documents): spec decisions

19 October 2026. Written by the spec session for `3-payslips-spec.md`. Each row is a question Paper left open or that production needs answered, my recommendation (already written into the spec), why, and what changing it takes. The spec cites rows as PD1, PD2 and so on. Rows marked **Owner** repeat an owner decision so the spec can point at it. Nothing here is built yet. The owner can change any row.

## The payslip code and who is told

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PD1 | What is "their own code"? | The person's own **4-digit PIN**, the same one managers sign with. Staff-facing words say "code"; manager dialogs say "PIN". A person with none sees "Choose your code" (the kit's first-use set dialog) before the first payslip. HR can reset a forgotten one (existing People action); after a reset the next opening asks for a new code. | One secret to remember. Paper draws four dots. | A separate credential table and a second set-up screen. |
| PD2 | How long does a correct code unlock? | One correct code unlocks **My payslips on that phone** (list, detail, document, download). It locks again when the person leaves My payslips, when the app has been in the background for **60 seconds**, or after **10 minutes** without a tap on a payslip screen. This is a screen lock, not a sign-out (Owner: nobody is signed out for inactivity). | Legacy behaviour was 3 minutes idle and tab blur; 10 and 60 s suit a phone. | Two constants. |
| PD3 | Wrong code | Every try is **recorded** (who, phone, time, never the digits). **Nobody is locked out** (Owner). After the 5th wrong try in 15 minutes the server answers each next try only after a growing pause (2, 4, 8 up to 30 seconds): a pause, never a lock. After the **3rd** wrong try in 15 minutes the **System Admin** and the person are told, once per 15-minute window (Owner, pass 4). | A 4-digit code with no limit can be guessed in an hour; a pause is not a lockout. | The pause schedule. |
| PD4 | Unrecognised phone | The first sign-in on a phone asks "Is this your phone?" (built elsewhere). An opening on an **unconfirmed** phone still works after the correct code, is recorded with `unconfirmed: true`, the person gets a loud push and the **System Admin** is told (Owner; Paper step 30 still says the Director). The screens never say how phones are recognised (Owner). | Pass 4. | Audience in the wording table. |
| PD5 | HR or the Accountant opening someone else's payslip | Allowed with `payrun.read` from the desktop Payslips tab (Segment 2 draws the tab); no staff code. Every opening is recorded with the opener's name and role. **The Director is told** (a Notifications row, never figures), **one row per opener per day** with a count ("HR opened 12 payslips today"). The staff member sees each opening under Activity on my record. | The brief names the Director for this case; one row per day stops a flood during review. | Per-opening rows instead of a daily row. |
| PD6 | Staff phone copy "This payslip is Cynthia Wairimu's" | Written "This payslip is {Name}, {Role}'s." and "Every opening is recorded with the phone and the time"; "the Director is told too" becomes "the System Admin is told too". | Naming rule (name plus role) and PD4. | Copy only. |
| PD7 | The old password gate | Replaced. The legacy gate re-asks the **account password** in the browser only; the server does not enforce it. The new gate is enforced by the server with a short-lived unlock token tied to the person and the phone. | A gate that only the page enforces protects nothing. | None. |

## Amounts, versions and notices

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PD8 | Hidden amounts | **Hidden by default**, one eye on the header, one preference **per device and per person** (local storage), applying to home, one payslip, year to date, history and the recent list. Amounts are not placed in the page while hidden. The document view and the PDF are not hidden (they sit behind the code). Step 36's detail gets the same eye (Paper omits it). | Owner. Screen readers must never read hidden amounts. | Moving the preference to the server. |
| PD9 | Version 2 for staff | A correction creates **version 2**; version 1 stays on record. The list row says "Version 2 · corrected 20 Oct". The detail shows "Earlier version (replaced)" which opens version 1 in the Document view with a diagonal "REPLACED" watermark and the line "Replaced by version 2 on 20 Oct 2026." A changed line is marked "corrected from 410" (as Paper step 36). Staff may download both. | Paper shows the replaced line only. | Watermark and one link. |
| PD10 | Figures in notices | **No pay figure in any push, Inbox row, notification or audit row.** Paper step 36's notice "net pay is KES 28,145 (it was 27,940)" becomes "Your payslip is now version 2. Open it to see what changed." | Brief and owner rule: lock-screen previews leak pay. | Copy only; the owner approved Paper's wording, so this is flagged. |
| PD11 | "Questions? Call HR" | The number comes from a new **company-wide field `hrContact { phone, hoursText }` in the Rules store**, shown and changed as one row on **Rules, Leave tab** ("Who staff phone about leave and pay", HR changes it, padlock for others, not dated). The 1A spec already points at this "HR contact line". The button is a `tel:` link on a phone; on a desktop it shows the number. Not set: the button is replaced by the text "Questions? Ask HR." | HR owns the Leave tab; no new tab. | A different tab or a Company setting. |
| PD12 | A person on the old payslip system | Per-site switch-on (the Nyeri Town pilot). My payslips always opens the new screens. If the person's site is not switched on and they have old payslips: one card "Your payslips are still on the old screen" with **Open old payslips** (the legacy page, its own gate). No old payslips: the empty state "Your first payslip will be here once your branch moves to the new payslips." After switch-on, History ends with a row "Before October 2026" that opens the legacy page until the old system is deleted. | No broken screen during rollout. | Remove the card when the old page is deleted. |

## The P9 and the documents

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PD13 | When is the P9 available? | For year Y: from the later of **1 January of Y+1** and the day **December of Y is marked Paid**. For a person who left during Y: from the day their final month is Marked Paid (HR gives it to them from the exited person's file, since a leaver has no login). A person who joined mid-year: the year's other months are blank rows marked "Not employed". | Paper: "ready in January 2027". | The availability rule. |
| PD14 | Who vouches for the P9 layout? | The **Accountant confirms the P9 format once** (a PIN confirm like the Statutory tab, kept in the Rules store as `p9FormatConfirmed {by, at}`). Until then the P9 button is disabled for everyone with "Waiting for the Accountant to confirm the P9 format." | The brief: the spec must not state tax rules as fact. | The gate flag. |
| PD15 | Document numbers | Payslip **PS-0987**, casual pay sheet **CPS-0318**, P9 **P9-2026-0214** (year, then the employee number's digits). Issued from sequences when the document is **published, approved or generated**; gaps allowed; version 2 keeps PS-0987. A draft shows "CPS-draft". | Matches 1A D12; version 2 must be the same number. | Sequence names. |
| PD16 | Frozen snapshot | Publishing stores a **frozen snapshot** of every figure and the rules versions used, and a file hash. Every download and share is the **same stored file** (byte-identical). A correction stores a new snapshot as version 2. A paid month never changes. The P9 is built from the latest version of each month's snapshot. | 1A D14, D16. | None. |
| PD17 | QR | `https://<app host>/verify/ps/PS-0987?h=<first 10 characters of the hash>` (same form for `cps` and `p9`). The page (Chapter 17) shows number, month, version and "Current" or "Replaced by version 2", the employer name; never a person's name or a figure. | 1A D15. | Page content. |
| PD18 | Who signs a payslip | Version 1 shows the **Accountant's** approval signature and PIN time; version 2 shows the signer of the correction (HR or the Accountant) and "Version 2, replaces version 1 of 19 Oct 2026". Staff never sign a payslip. | Paper step 32; leave slips are not signed by staff (design pass 1). | None. |

## Casual pay

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PD19 | Who is a casual worker? | A person with employment type **CASUAL**: no login, no payslip, no file in Employees beyond a short record (full name, national ID number, phone optional, department, site), paid in cash each day with no statutory deductions (Rules, Casual work). | Rules card. | A fuller file. |
| PD20 | How a head adds one | "+ Add a casual" on the Casuals screen: first time, full name and ID number (asked twice, matched); after that the person is picked from "My casuals" for the department. The ID number is unique across the company, so a casual who works at two sites is **one record**. The sheet shows the ID masked ("ID 3••••••21"). HR can read and correct the record. | One person, one record. | None. |
| PD21 | One pay sheet is what? | One **department, at one site, for one day**, numbered CPS-n at approval. A department has one open draft per day (shared by its heads). | Paper CPS-0318 is Service, Nyeri Town, Mon 19 Oct. | Group by week. |
| PD22 | The day's pay | Daily rate for the person's department **in force on the work date** (Rules, Casual work, versioned). Hours up to 8 pay `rate × hours ÷ 8`; hours over 8 pay `rate ÷ 8` each; rounded to the **whole shilling** (half up). A full 8-hour day shows exactly the day rate (900). Lateness needs no rule: the hours worked are the recorded times. The Accountant must confirm the rounding before go-live. | Rules table shows "per hour over 8"; the shorter day is not drawn. | Minimum one day, or the rounding. |
| PD23 | Recording rules | The **department head** records (`casual.record`, own department) for the **last 3 days and today**, never the future. A sheet can be **sent** only when every worker's end time has passed ("Rose Wanjiru's shift ends at 19:00"). Times are 24-hour, start before end (past midnight shows "+1"), at most 16 hours. Duplicate: a worker with overlapping times on any site that day is refused with the other site and times (no names of the other head). | Paper records 11:00 to 19:00 at 14:40; production cannot pay for hours not yet worked. | Window and limit. |
| PD24 | Who approves, who holds the cash | The **Branch Manager** of the site approves with a PIN, never the person who recorded it (a Branch Manager who is also the head sends it to the Director). Cash is handed out by the **cash holder** the Rules name (the Branch Manager or one named person), never the head (Rules). The approval line names the holder; a named holder who is not the approver is told. | Rules, Casual work; Paper step 34's line assumes the Branch Manager holds the cash. | Another approver. |
| PD25 | How a worker's receipt is taken | After approval the head's phone lists each worker "Waiting for the cash". When the holder has paid, the worker types their **full name** on the head's phone and taps "I received KES 900"; the printed sheet shows the name in the script font and the time. A sheet printed before the signatures shows a blank signature line instead (works with no signal). A worker unsigned after 3 days is listed for the Branch Manager. | Paper shows signatures at 19:05 on the head's phone; the cash holder and the head are different people. | A drawn signature. |
| PD26 | A day recorded wrongly | Before approval: the head edits or takes the sheet back; the Branch Manager can "Send back" with a reason. After approval and **before any worker has signed**: the Branch Manager **voids** it (reason, PIN); the VOID number stays on record and the head records a new sheet (new number). After a signature: nothing is edited; the head adds a **correction line** (plus or minus, with a reason) on a later sheet, approved the usual way. | Keeps every signed paper true. | Allow editing after signing. |
| PD27 | Memo line on payroll | Approved sheets of a month are a **memo line** in Payroll (the Casual pay tab, Segment 2): per site and department, approved total only, shown beside the totals and **outside** gross, net and the bank file. Waiting sheets are listed separately. Paid from petty cash. | Owner: not in the bank file. | None. |
| PD28 | Head with no signal | The head can record, edit and preview with no signal: the sheet is **saved on the phone** (states kit marker) and sends by itself when the signal returns; it is not sent to the Branch Manager until the head taps Send. | Branch phones lose signal in the store room. | None. |
| PD29 | Two heads, one worker, one day | The draft is shared and versioned: a second head sees the first head's entries; a stale save answers "This changed while you were looking. Check it again." A worker already recorded in another department's sheet at overlapping times is refused (PD23). | Idempotent, versioned writes (1A D25). | None. |
