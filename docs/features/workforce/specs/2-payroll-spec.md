## 0. Rules that apply to every screen

- **Frame and kit.** Everything in 1A section 0 applies unchanged (frame, colour-by-token table, type, buttons, times, naming, URL state style, the list of every-screen production additions: loading, empty, error, offline, refusal in the server's words, focus, reduced motion, long names, 200 people, narrow widths, double taps). Import from `features/workforce/_shared`; the page lives at `app/app/workforce/payroll/` (the nav row `wf-pay-runs` is renamed Payroll, `newHref /app/workforce/payroll`, `notBuilt` off). The shell adds nothing role-specific.
- **Three new kit pieces** (add to `_shared`, then compose): `StageLine` (S-shell), `MonthsList` (extracted from `timesheets/months`, parameterised by row tag and sub-line, so Timesheets and Payroll share one months list), `EstimateBar` (S10). Everything else is composed from the kit: `PageHeader size="lg"`, `KpiStrip`, `UnderlineTabs`, `StatusTag`, `Callout`, `DataTable tableLook="workforce"`, `Panel`, `SettingRows`, `StatBoxes`, `ChecklistRows`, `KeyValueRows`, `KitDialog plain`, `WorkforcePinDialog`, `PinField`, `FormField`, `RadioCards`, `ChoiceChips`, `WorkforceDrawer`, the states kit, `useRemote`/`useAction`, `WorkforceDocument`.
- **Money.** KES amounts are Geist Mono 13/16, whole shillings with thousands separators and no currency sign (the column or label says KES); Excel and the bank file carry two decimals (E5 rounding). Zero shows `0` in `neutral-500`. Never a float anywhere: amounts travel as decimal strings.
- **Who sees what** (E9 has the full table): HR and the Accountant work; the Director and System Admin read; a Branch or Store Manager gets the kit "no access" page (403, no menu link). The Director's reading view is the same page, with every action absent.
- **No pay figure in a notification, a toast or the URL.** Toasts say what happened ("September sent to the Accountant"), never an amount.
- **Month in the URL:** `/app/workforce/payroll?month=2026-09&tab=timesheets|rules|tax|casual|totals|payslips&site=&q=&page=&rows=`; default month = the oldest month that is not Paid (the one that needs someone), else the latest; a person's correction dialog adds `&payslip=<id>`.

**How "earned so far" is computed with no approved timesheets (P28, E7).** From the pay profile, the employment dates and the authorised overtime items so far, never from payslips: **Earned so far** = Σ over people employed at an included site of `basic × (days employed from the 1st to yesterday ÷ days in the month)` plus the value of overtime **authorised so far** (decided items only), minus unpaid absence days to date. **Expected** = Σ `basic for the days the person will be employed in the month` plus authorised overtime so far scaled by `days in month ÷ days elapsed`. **Vs September** = expected minus last month's calculated (or final) gross, per site. Gross only: no statutory lines on the open month (they appear from Calculated). "To decide" overtime is valued at the person's hourly rate × ordinary multiplier for the callout figure and is never in the totals. The percentages are `earned ÷ expected`, and the marker is `days elapsed ÷ days in month` (Paper draws both at 61%: the fill equals the marker only when pay accrues evenly; a month with extra overtime pushes the fill past the marker). Refreshed nightly by a job at 00:30 Nairobi (Paper's "18:00 · refreshed every night" is inconsistent; the card says `Updated {date}, {time} · refreshed every night` with the real time); a manual refresh is not offered.

**Production adds.** Before the first nightly run: `Not worked out yet. It appears after tonight's update.` A month in its first days (day 1): the bar shows 0%, `Today is 1 Oct, day 1 of 31`. A site not on the new system (E10): its row shows `Not on the new system yet` in `neutral-600` with `—` figures and is not in the totals (the Total row's caption reads `4 sites, 1 not on the new system`). Joiners and leavers inside the month are prorated by calendar days; a person with no pay profile is not in the figures and the callout adds `{n} people have no pay profile and are not included.` Loading and error as the shell. An estimate is **never exported** (the Export menu is disabled for an Open month with `Nothing is calculated yet.`). Screen reader: the bar is a `meter`-like `img` with the text "Earned 61 percent of expected; today is 61 percent of the month"; the table has a caption.

**Server.** `GET .../months/:monthKey/estimate` → `{asOf, computedAt, dayOfMonth, daysInMonth, expectedGross, earnedSoFar, vsLastMonth {amount, percent}, bySite [{siteId, name, people, earned, expected, vsLast, onNewSystem}], toDecideOvertime {minutes, value, site?}, missingProfiles}`; capability `payroll.read`; stored by the nightly job (`pay_month_estimates`), read from the store (never computed in the request).
