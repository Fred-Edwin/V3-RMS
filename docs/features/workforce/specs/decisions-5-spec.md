# Segment 5 (Conduct): spec decisions

20 October 2026. Written by the spec session for `5-conduct-spec.md`. Each row is a question Paper or the old code left open, my recommendation (already written into the spec), why, and what changing it takes. The spec cites rows as C1, C2 and so on. Rows marked **Owner** repeat an owner decision. Nothing here is built. The owner can change any row. Wording and figures that touch employment law are placeholders for the client's lawyer; nothing here states law as fact.

## Coexistence, levels and letters

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Which system handles a person's conduct during rollout? | **By site, with the Attendance switch** (`AttendanceSiteSwitch.enabled`; pilot Nyeri Town). A site with the switch on issues new notices only in the new Conduct; a site with it off stays on the old HR disciplinary screen. The old `POST /hr/disciplinary` and `POST /hr/disciplinary/:id/acknowledge` get a small additive guard that refuses for a switched-on site (`409 CONDUCT_IN_NEW_SYSTEM`); the old list stays readable. Legacy tables untouched. | The facts on a notice come from the new timesheets, which only exist where the switch is on. One switch for the pilot, the same rule the payroll guard follows. | A second per-site switch table and a second System Admin screen. |
| C2 | What happens to existing old records? | They stay in `disciplinary_records`, never copied or edited. The new staff Conduct page and the employee file Conduct tab show them as read-only rows marked "Before the new system" (verbal, written, final warnings; suspension and termination shown as "Other action"). They never count towards the ladder suggestion. | A copy would invent signatures and dates the old rows do not hold. | A one-off import that creates ended notices without letters. |
| C3 | The ladder. | Three levels, fixed in code: Verbal note, Written warning, Final written warning. Suspension and termination are not notices and stay outside the system (the old screen keeps them for sites still on it). | Paper draws three levels; the Rules tab holds three. | New level codes in `ConductRulesSchema` and the letters file. |
| C4 | Where does letter wording live? | Developer constants in `backend/src/modules/workforce/conduct/letters/conduct-letters.ts`, one template per level, with blanks (`{firstName}`, `{dates}`, `{minutes}`, `{validUntil}`, `{appealBy}`, `{ownLine}`). The text in the spec is **placeholder wording copied from Paper, to be replaced by the lawyer's text**. A version string (`letterVersion`) is stored on each notice. | Owner decision: fixed letters, no editing screen. | Edit the file, bump `letterVersion`; issued notices keep the old text (they are frozen). |
| C5 | Which facts are attached? | About **Lateness** or **Absence**: the unexcused days from the timesheets for the month the manager picks (default the last completed month; the last 3 months are offered), copied into the notice. **Behaviour, Safety or hygiene, Other**: no timesheet facts; the manager's own line is required (20 to 300 characters). For Lateness and Absence the own line is also required. | Paper shows only the Lateness case. A letter without facts for behaviour would be an empty accusation. | Add fact sources per category. |
| C6 | Own line. | One required box, 20 to 300 characters, printed in the letter in its own paragraph. The rest of the letter cannot be edited. The manager sees a live preview ("Read the letter" link opens the Document view of the draft with the label "Draft, not issued"). | Paper says "one or two sentences". A preview stops surprises. | Length limits in one constant. |
| C7 | Which level may be issued? | The screen suggests: no active notice, Verbal note; active verbal note, Written warning; active written warning, Final written warning; active final, "A final written warning is already on file. Speak to HR." and the Sign button is disabled. The manager may choose a different level, except **Final written warning needs an active Written warning on the file** (button disabled with that reason). Expired, withdrawn and replaced notices never count. A reduced notice counts at its new level. | Paper: "A final warning needs an earlier written warning". | One function `suggestLevel`. |
| C8 | Who may issue, and who decides an appeal? | Issue: Branch Manager (own site), Store Manager (Central Store), HR (any site, including a Branch Manager). Decide appeals: HR. **Exception: an appeal on a notice HR issued is decided by the Director** (Paper card "Warnings for managers"). Nobody issues to themself, decides their own notice, or confirms as witness for their own notice. The Director only reads. | The issuer never judges their own notice. | One check in the appeal service. |
| C9 | A person with no login (a casual). | Can be issued a notice. The issue dialog warns "This person has no login. Print the letter, give it by hand, then record that it was given." The sign step does not exist for them and "Record that it was not signed" is available at once (reason chip "They have no login"). | Casuals are real and are also disciplined. | Make notices login-only. |

## Signing and not signing

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C10 | What is "their own code"? | The person's PIN, same as payslips (PD1). | One secret. | A new credential. |
| C11 | A wrong code. **Owner** (pass 4). | Every try is recorded (who, phone, time, never the digits). Nobody is locked out. After the 5th wrong try in 15 minutes the server answers each next try after a pause (2, 4, 8 up to 30 seconds). After the 3rd wrong try in 15 minutes the **System Admin** and the person are told, once per 15 minutes. Reuses the payslip code machinery (PD3). | Same rule everywhere. | The pause schedule. |
| C12 | Can a person sign late? | Yes, any time while the notice is active. Signing after "not signed" was recorded changes the headline to Signed and keeps both events in the record ("Signed late"). Signing never extends the appeal days. | A person who finally reads it should be able to say so. | Lock signing after the 3 days. |
| C13 | Not signed: what is recorded? | Open from the day after the sign limit (or at once for a person with no login). Reason chips: they refused; I could not reach them; they have no login. A **witness is required**: another person on the same site, not the issuer and not the subject. The issuer signs the record **with their PIN** (Paper step 9 has no PIN; added because it is a signed record under a name). The witness is asked on their phone to confirm with their own code. A witness who has not confirmed in 3 days does **not** block the notice; HR sees "Witness not confirmed" on the notice. | Paper: "records why, with a witness". An unconfirmed witness is a record, not a reason to void a notice. | Block until confirmed. |
| C14 | Is the sign button before or after reading? | The button is enabled at once; "Read the full letter" opens the Document view. | Paper step 2. Forcing a read cannot be proved. | Require the viewer to reach the end. |

## Appeals

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C15 | Appeal rules. | One appeal per notice, within the Rules `appealWithinDays` (7), counted from the issue date and ending at 23:59 Nairobi on the 7th day, signed or not (**Owner**). A reason chip plus a written reason of 10 to 1,000 characters is required. An appeal before signing is allowed. After the window the button is gone and the page says "The 7 days to appeal ended on Fri 16 Oct. Speak to HR." No second appeal. | Paper and the owner's design pass. | `appealWithinDays` in Rules. |
| C16 | Decision outcomes. | **Keep the warning**, **Reduce** to a lower level (from Written: Verbal note; from Final: Written warning or Verbal note), **Withdraw**. A reason (10 to 1,000 characters, shown to the staff member) and the PIN are required. The decision is final. | Paper step 11. | More outcomes. |
| C17 | What does a reduction create? | A new notice linked to the old one (`replacesNoticeId`). A new Verbal note has no letter and no number, and its end date is the decision date plus the Verbal months (Paper: 12 Oct plus 3 months is 12 Jan 2027). A new Written warning gets a **new WRN number** and a letter built from the fixed Written template with the original facts and the line "Reduced on appeal from a final written warning", signed by HR with PIN, needing no receipt signature and giving no new appeal. The old notice becomes Replaced on appeal. | Paper step 4. A reduced written letter needs a number so it can be checked. | Reduce only to a verbal note. |
| C18 | What happens to the old notice document? | **Versions, never edited.** Version 1 (as issued) is frozen and stays byte-identical. After a decision, version 2 is the same page 1 plus a second page "Decision on appeal" (outcome, reason, decided by name and role, signed with PIN, time). Download gives the latest version; HR, the issuer and the Director can also open "As issued". | Same pattern as a corrected payslip. | Always one page. |
| C19 | HR changing a notice without an appeal. | The notice detail has "Change this notice" for HR (or the Director for notices HR issued): the same decision dialog with only Reduce and Withdraw, reason and PIN. Allowed any time while active or ended. Beyond Paper; the design pass says HR can withdraw or change any notice and the manager has no request button (the manager phones HR). | A real mistake must be fixable. | Remove the action. |
| C20 | HR's time to decide. | 7 days from the appeal, a **constant** (`HR_DECISION_DAYS`) because the Rules Conduct tab has no row for it. On day 8 undecided, the Director is told (a "Needs you" card on the Director's Home and one Inbox row, reminders only). | Paper step 10 and 13 state 7 days and the Director. | Add a Rules row (Director edits). |

## Expiry, hearing, document, access

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C21 | How long is a level active? | The Rules Conduct tab as built: 3, 6 and 12 months. The notice stores `validUntil` (issue date plus months, calendar months, clamped to month end) and the days to sign and appeal **at issue** (D29). The notice is active through its `validUntil` day and Ended from 00:00 Nairobi the next day (derived at read time; a daily job at 00:10 Nairobi sets the stored status and writes the event). | Paper: "9 Apr 2027 and then ends". A rules change must never alter an issued notice. | Change the job time. |
| C22 | The hearing after a final written warning. | **Left outside the system in this segment.** The Final written warning letter carries a fixed placeholder line that HR will invite the person to a hearing before any further step. HR files the hearing minutes in the employee file's Documents tab titled "Hearing record WRN-nnnn". No screen, no status. | A half-built hearing would suggest a legal process the lawyer has not confirmed. Paper draws none (logged since pass 1). | A simple Hearing record on the notice (date, who attended, outcome note, PIN) in a later slice. |
| C23 | Notice number. | `WRN-` plus the number from a database sequence (`conduct_notice_seq`), padded to 4 digits, issued when a Written or Final notice is issued (gaps allowed, like TSH). Verbal notes have no number. A reduced Written notice takes a new number. | Matches TSH-n. | Per-site series. |
| C24 | What does the QR carry? | `{app origin}/verify/WRN-0007?c={code}`, where `code` is a random 12-character code stored on the notice. The verify page (Segment 6) returns only: number, level, issue date, site name, and the current headline (Valid, Replaced on appeal, Withdrawn, Ended). Never the person's name, the facts or the reason. | The page must prove a paper is real without leaking a staff file. | Show more on the verify page. |
| C25 | "Mr" in the letter, and gendered words. | The letter opens "Dear {first name}," and the address block shows the full name and the role and site, with no title. Paper's "Mr" is dropped. | Owner rule: no he, she, her, his. | Add a title field to the people file. |
| C26 | Who sees what. | See section 12 of the spec. A department head sees nothing (Paper's "Told, then no access" row is changed to "Nothing unless named as a witness"). The staff member reads only their own notices. The Branch Manager reads notices of people at their site now **and** notices issued by their site. | Design pass 4: heads approve nothing and see nothing. A new Branch Manager must see a transferred person's active notices for the ladder. | A stricter scope. |
| C27 | Notifications. | The push and the lock-screen text are generic: "You have a message in your Inbox." The Inbox row (inside the app) names the level and the number but never the manager's own line or the facts. HR rows name person and number. | The lock screen can be seen by others. | One flag per message in the wording table. |
| C28 | Letters are English only. | English now. A Swahili version waits for the lawyer. | Paper is English. | A second constants file per language and a language field. |
