# Segment 6 (Shared parts): spec decisions

20 October 2026. Written by the spec session for `6a-audit-and-security-spec.md`, `6b-needs-you-and-messages-spec.md` and `6c-documents-and-verify-spec.md`. Each row is a question Paper left open or that production needs answered: the choice (already written into the specs), why, and what changing it takes. The specs cite rows as SD1, SD2 and so on. Rows marked **Owner** repeat an owner decision so the specs can point at it. Nothing here is built. The owner can change any row.

## Coexistence with the old system

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SD1 | How do the new audit log, "Needs you", alerts, messages and verify page live beside the old code? | **Additive, per site.** Nothing old is deleted by this segment. The new parts read only the new tables and the new screens. The old HR dashboard list ("awaiting review", `hr/page.tsx`), the old leave page and the old Inbox stay exactly as they are for a site whose new clock is off. A site that is switched on (the Attendance switch, System Admin only, Nyeri Town first) gets the new Home cards and badges; the sources of "Needs you" skip legacy sites so nothing is counted twice. The only edits to old code are three small additive guards: the error handler adds a `reference` to a 5xx answer, the Inbox page gets one Notifications tab, and the signer PIN stops locking (SD16). | A rollout that cannot break a working branch. | Deleting the old HR list is a later clean-up once every site is on. |
| SD2 | One company log or one per feature? **Owner:** one. | One table, `workforce_audit_entries`, gains a nullable-with-default `area` column (`WORKFORCE`; later `INVENTORY`, `ACCESS`) and a `flagged` boolean. **Area** (Workforce, Inventory, Access) is the filter in Paper; **Event** is the second filter (People, Time, Leave, Pay, Rules, Security, Documents, Conduct), taken from the category. | Paper has both dropdowns; the chain stays one chain per company. | Splitting the table per area breaks the one chain. |
| SD3 | What does the Area filter list today? | Only areas that have entries. Today that is Workforce and Access (sign-ins, phones, security). Inventory keeps its own derived log at `/app/inventory/audit-log` until Inventory is next touched and writes through the same writer, then its area appears here. | Do not show an empty Inventory area, and do not rewrite Inventory in a Workforce segment. | Nothing: the area appears by itself when rows exist. |
| SD4 | Who reads which area? | The access table in `workforce-access.ts` stays the only authority (read per category). Three grants are missing against Paper and are added: HR reads `rules_operating` and `rules_pay` (Paper: "every rule change, read only"); the Accountant reads `pay_run_prepare` (publishing, corrections, exports); the Director and the System Admin get a new `audit.verify_chain`. | Paper step 5 says so; the table disagrees today. | A line in the table and its test. |
| SD5 | Retention. **Owner:** 7 years. | Entries are never deleted in this segment. The screen and the verify page show the last 7 years; older rows read "archived" and are reachable only by the System Admin's yearly export. The yearly archive job (partition and checkpoint hash) is a ship-preparation item. | The database refuses deletes; a checkpoint is needed before any archive. | The cut-off constant. |

## Audit log

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SD6 | A very large log. | The table convention (`UI_BUILD_RULES` §4a): search and filters first, type-ahead, numbered pager with rows per page (25, 50, 100, default 50), state in the URL. The total is exact up to 50,000 matches and reads "over 50,000" beyond, with the hint "Narrow the dates to count them". Paper's "Previous · Page 1 of 143 · Next" footer is **not** built (the older Paper pages keep the old footer by owner rule). | Counting a million rows on every page is slow; the Director still needs the size of a narrowed list. | The cap constant. |
| SD7 | Export. | "Export this view" downloads a UTF-8 CSV of exactly the filtered view (the reader's allowed areas only), at most 50,000 rows, and writes `audit.exported` (who, filters, row count). Over the cap the button says "Too many to export. Narrow the dates." | Paper has the button, no format. | A PDF export is a new document. |
| SD8 | Tamper evidence. | The entry drawer shows the entry number (seq) and the first 10 characters of its hash and of the previous hash. The Director and the System Admin get **Check the chain**, which re-checks a date range on the server and answers "Chain intact" or "Entry 4,182 does not match". A nightly job checks the last day and raises a System Admin alert if it fails. | The table and trigger exist; the proof must be visible. | None. |
| SD9 | "Who looked at this log" (Paper step 1 side panel). | Director only. One `audit.log_viewed` entry per reader per 10 minutes, not one per page click. The panel lists the last 10 readers. | A per-click entry floods the log. | The window constant. |
| SD10 | What is "flagged"? | A registry rule per action: three wrong payslip codes, a payslip opened on an unconfirmed phone, "This wasn't me", a chain failure, a wrong signing PIN the 3rd time, a corrected payslip after the bank file. Stored as `flagged` on the entry so it filters and indexes. | Paper tints these rows. | The registry. |
| SD11 | Activity on my record chips. | Paper's All, Pay, Time, Leave, Sign-ins, plus **File** and **Conduct** (production adds). "Who looked at the log" is never shown there (built rule). | A file edit and a notice are things done to the person. | The chip list. |

## Security alerts and phones

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SD12 | Where does the System Admin see alerts? **Owner:** the System Admin, not the Director. | A new table `workforce_security_alerts` and a menu row **Security alerts** under Settings (badge `workforce-security`), plus a Needs-you row on the System Admin's Home. An alert stays open until the System Admin clears it with a one-line note. The Director does not get the alert list; the Director reads the same facts in the Audit log. | Owner. A clearable list is what an alert needs. | Another audience. |
| SD13 | When is an alert raised? | Four kinds: **This wasn't me**; **Payslip opened on an unconfirmed phone**; **3 wrong payslip codes** (once per 15 minutes, PD3); **Audit chain check failed**. A phone the person answered "Use it just once" counts as confirmed for that session. The unconfirmed-phone push stays held for a site until its staff have all been asked "Is this your phone?" once (rollout). | Without this, every first week would be noise. | A flag per site. |
| SD14 | What does "This wasn't me" do? | Signs out every session of the person, requires a new password at the next sign-in and a new payslip code, marks the phone and the entry, raises the alert, writes the entry. Nobody else's data changes. | Paper step 4. | None. |
| SD15 | How are phones recognised? | **Developer notes only** (6a section P). Not in any copy or UI section. | **Owner.** | None. |
| SD16 | Signing PIN. **Owner:** nobody is ever locked out for wrong codes. | The built signer PIN locks 10 minutes after 5 wrong tries and keeps the count in memory (`signer-pin.ts`). Replace it: every wrong try is recorded; from the 5th in 15 minutes an answer waits 2, 4, 8, 16, then 30 seconds; a pause, never a lock; the count is stored, so a restart does not clear it. | A locked Accountant at month end is a payroll stop. | The pause schedule. |

## Needs you

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SD17 | What is "Needs you" and how is it different from "Needs your attention"? | **Needs you** = an answer someone is waiting for (a decision, an approval, a step). **Needs your attention** (HR's Home) = housekeeping with no one waiting (finish hiring, probation ends, no head). Leave and Report-a-problem rows move into Needs you. The KPI cell "Waiting for you" becomes "Needs you" and scrolls to the card. | One meaning each. | Card ids. |
| SD18 | What do the row colours mean? | The **Since** text is neutral today, amber from yesterday, red after 3 days. A row with the amber fill and bold text is **new since the last time this person opened Home**. Paper uses the fill for the newest row in the Branch Manager card and for the 3-day row in HR's; one meaning is built. | Paper is not consistent. | Copy and colour. |
| SD19 | How do badges count? | A badge on a menu link counts the rows that open that link's feature: Timesheets (overtime, clock fixes, Report a problem, month approval, casual pay sheets), Leave, Conduct, Payroll, Security alerts. Home carries no badge. | Matches the built `workforce-timesheets` badge and Paper's "Timesheets 3". Casual approval has no link of its own for the Branch Manager. | Add a link. |
| SD20 | Refresh. | A socket nudge `workforce:needs-you` with no counts (the Inventory pattern); the open screen refetches. Also a refetch on tab focus and every 60 seconds while visible. An answered row leaves at once with a 30 second undo (rule 03). | Works with a lost socket. | The interval. |
| SD21 | The Director's Home. **Owner:** reminders only. | Three cards: **Overdue** (anything past 3 days anywhere, and a site not approved after the 5th, with Send a reminder), **Needs you** (only the Director's own decisions: Head office timesheets, a casual sheet a Branch Manager recorded), and **Payslip openings** (SD30). A card with no rows is not drawn. | The Director decides little. | Rows. |
| SD22 | Money in a row. | A Needs-you row never shows a pay figure. Paper's "net KES 3,168,500" in the Accountant's row becomes "86 people". | A Home screen is photographed and shared. | One word. |
| SD23 | How are people "told"? | The built notifier only logs. It becomes real: a `workforce_notifications` table, an FCM push (title and body from `notification-copy.ts`, never a figure), the socket nudge, and a **Notifications** tab inside the existing Inbox page (the old tabs unchanged). The Inbox badge adds the unread count. Loud kinds (unconfirmed phone, 3 wrong codes, This wasn't me) ignore quiet hours. | Without it no "told" row in any approved segment arrives. | A separate screen. |
| SD24 | Reminders. | A reminder is a notification to the person the item waits on (and a log entry). One per item per day. It never decides anything. | **Owner** (Director sends reminders only). | The limit. |

## Documents and verify

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SD25 | Is the verify page public? | **Public**, no sign-in. It confirms genuine only when the number **and** the hash prefix in the QR match, and shows the type, number, period, version state, signer **role** and signing date. Never a person's name, a figure, a head count or a site. Signing in adds the signer's name and the Open button. Paper shows the signer's name publicly; the spec shows the role. | A stranger holding a printed copy must learn nothing they do not hold. | Show the name. |
| SD26 | Not found, wrong hash, replaced, voided. | One public answer for unknown number and wrong hash: "This copy does not match our records. Do not rely on it." (same wording and timing, so a number cannot be probed). Replaced: genuine but "Replaced by version 2 on 20 Oct". Withdrawn or voided: "Withdrawn on {date} by {role}". Archived after 7 years. A draft carries no QR. | Paper step 2 and the "decided" card. | Copy. |
| SD27 | Rate limit. | 30 checks a minute and 300 a day per address, then "Too many checks. Try again in a minute." | Cheap guard. | The numbers. |
| SD28 | What is in the Signed documents index? | A read model filled by one `registerDocument` door the issuers call in their own transaction (payroll summary and register PAY, payslip PS, casual sheet CPS, P9, timesheet TSH; later leave slip LVE, warning WRN, contract CTR). **Weekly rota ROT is not listed**: the rota has no sign step. A type shows only to roles that may read it (the Director does not see payslips PS; HR and the Accountant do). | Paper lists a rota and a payslip for the Director against the access table. | Add a signature to the rota. |
| SD29 | "Copy link". | Copies the verify URL. | The one stable link. | None. |
| SD30 | The Director's payslip-opens digest. | One row per opener per day ("HR opened 12 payslips today"), on the Director's Home card and as a filter of the log. Never whose payslip, never an amount. `GET /payslips/opens` gains `from` and `to` and stays compatible with its `day`. | Built to PD5. | None. |
