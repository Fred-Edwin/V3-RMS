# Workforce 4 (Leave, back end): decisions

Branch `feat/workforce-4-leave-backend`, 20 Oct 2026. Back end only. Each row: the question, what was chosen, why, and what changing it takes. Owner decisions of 20 Oct 2026 (O1 annual leave in full at the start, O2 no PIN to decline and a PIN to adjust, O3 verify page shows role and date only, casual workers) are applied, not reopened.

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How does O1 map onto the Rules Leave tab? | The accrual mode of the type, read once on the grant date (1 January, or the joiner's start date), decides the year. `UP_FRONT` is the owner's model: the full figure on 1 January, prorated for a mid-year joiner by whole months left (the start month counts only when starting on the 1st), rounded down. `MONTHLY` still works if HR switches a type to it. | A mode change later in the year must never double-credit a year. | Change `entitlementFor` |
| 2 | Casual workers | A person on a daily rate without an active contract has no balance and no requests. Their overview answers 200 with `casual: true`, no cards and the message "Leave starts when your contract starts."; their list is empty; a send answers 409 `CASUAL_NO_LEAVE`; HR's adjust answers 409. Never a 404 or 500. | Owner decision; the screen shows a calm note. | Drop `isCasual` in `people-repository.ts` |
| 3 | Where does the idempotency key live? | Header `Idempotency-Key` (8 to 80 characters), required on ask, cancel, approve, decline and adjust. Ask: unique `(employee, key)` on the request. Approve, decline, cancel: the key is stored on the status event (unique `(application, key)`). Adjust: unique `(employee, key)` on the ledger. A repeat answers the first result with `repeat: true`. | Strict body schemas would reject a body field; one rule for all five. | Move the key into the body schemas |
| 4 | Two people deciding at once | A row lock (`FOR UPDATE`) plus `version` compare-and-swap inside the transaction. The loser gets 409 `ALREADY_DECIDED` (who, when, outcome) or 409 `STALE_REQUEST`. Two asks by one person take a per-person advisory lock so overlapping requests cannot both pass. | Never overwrite silently. | n/a |
| 5 | Nobody holds the routed role at the site | The request is routed to HR (stage HR) and the audit entry says so, so it never sits unseen. | A site with no active Branch Manager would otherwise strand the request for 72 hours. | Remove the fallback in `ask-service.ts` |
| 6 | One notification text for push and Inbox | The notifier renders one text per kind, so the Leave wording is generic ("A leave request needs you", "Your leave request has an answer") with no names, dates, balances or reasons. The detail is one tap away. | The spec's Inbox rows with names and balances cannot be built on a one-text notifier; the lock-screen rule wins. | Extend the notifier to render a separate Inbox body |
| 7 | Legacy waiting request approved in the new system | A carried request counted in working days is recounted in calendar days when approved, and becomes a normal new request. | One counting rule for everything that gets a slip (L6). | Keep the old count on the slip |
| 8 | Where is the department head told? | On approval only, by the "department head" notice; the head's login must exist. A head who is the person themself is not told. | Spec section 8. | n/a |
| 9 | Timesheets `leaveDays`, snapshot paid and unpaid days, payroll `UNPAID_ABSENCE` | **Not built in this session.** The brief's scope names the rota lookup, the Today board, "On leave" and "Coming up"; the timesheet snapshot and payroll input changes are a separate, riskier change in two finished segments. Logged as a gap. | Do not touch approved payroll maths without its own proof. | A small additive change in `timesheets/approval/snapshot.ts` and `payroll/calculate/load-inputs.ts` reading `approvedBetween` |
| 10 | Today board and "On leave" | The board tile reads the real count (`available: true`). Names come from `GET /workforce/leave/away`. The board's people list is unchanged. | Smallest change to a finished screen. | Add `onLeavePeople` to the board view |
| 11 | "Coming up" | Served by `GET /away` and the exported `comingUp` helper (approved leave starting within 14 days). The Home card is segment 6's. | Out of scope. | n/a |
| 12 | Heads and the Leave page | A department head may call `GET /away` (their departments only) but gets 403 on every Leave page list. | L34. | Grant `leave.read` at unit |
| 13 | Balances list size | At most 500 people are read, then filtered and paged in memory; rows per page is capped at 100. | A bounded call at 86 people today. | Move the paging into the query |
| 14 | Requests list "Recent" | Waiting, or asked or decided in the last 30 days, or not yet ended. The Needs-you tab is the same function as the badge and the Home card (`routedToMe`). | Spec section 9: the three must agree. | n/a |
| 15 | Minimum cover for a person with no department | No cover figure (never tight). | Nothing to compare. | n/a |
| 16 | System Admin | Fallback only: may decide only when nobody holds the deciding role; never gets a badge or a Needs-you row. | Section 5. | n/a |
| 17 | Slip PDF line wrapping | The three paragraphs wrap through the document's own text call (the shared one-line helper cuts text with an ellipsis). | Paragraphs are several lines long. | n/a |
| 18 | Documents door | It does not exist yet. `registerLeaveSlipDocument(register)` and `leaveSlipDocument` are exported and tested; `verifyLeaveSlip(number, hash)` returns role and date only. | Back end B builds the door. | Call `registerLeaveSlipDocument` at start-up once B exists |
| 19 | Switch-off mirror | Waiting or approved new requests that have not ended are inserted as marked old rows (comment "Mirrored from the new leave system") when an old balance row exists; others are counted as `notMirrored` in the audit entry, never guessed. The new row remembers the old id. | L5 without inventing balances. | Create the missing old balance row |
| 20 | Hourly sweep | `leave.escalation` on the report queue, every hour; it marks only waiting, unmarked rows, one transaction each. | The stage is derived, so a missed run only delays notices. | n/a |
