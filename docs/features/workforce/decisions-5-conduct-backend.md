# Conduct back end: decisions log

Written 20 Oct 2026 during the Segment 5 back end build. Each row: the question, what was chosen, why, and what changing it takes. Letter wording is placeholder text a lawyer must replace (one file: `conduct/letters/conduct-letters.ts`).

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who can be issued a notice without signing on a phone? (spec C9) | The rule keys on **having a login**, not on being a casual. Someone with no login gets the printed route: handed over, recorded as not signed with a witness. A casual with a login signs with their PIN like anyone. | Owner decision 20 Oct 2026. Casuals are not all login-less and some staff without a login are not casuals. | `issue-service.ts` (`personUserId`) and `not-signed-service.ts` (`NO_LOGIN` reason). |
| 2 | Does a notice during probation count toward the ladder? | Yes, like any other. It does not by itself end probation. | Owner decision 20 Oct 2026. | `conduct-ladder.ts` (`suggestLevel`) and `activeForEmployee`. |
| 3 | Needs-you rows: one summed row or one per notice? | One per notice, kinds `conduct-sign`, `conduct-record`, `conduct-appeal`. | The card opens the exact notice; a badge is the row count, so card and badge cannot disagree. | `needs-you/conduct-source.ts`. |
| 4 | How do staff get a menu badge, when they lack `needs_you.read`? | `GET /workforce/conduct/badge` counts the caller's own Conduct rows (capability `discipline.read_own`). One export added to `needs-you/index.ts` (`contextFor`). | Staff cannot call the shared badge endpoint. Conduct counts from its own source, so the number matches the card. | `needs-you/badge-service.ts`. |
| 5 | What does a push or lock-screen text say? | Generic text through a new optional `pushText`; Inbox rows name the level and number only. Never the reason, facts or own line. | Spec C27. A lock screen is public. | `notification-types.ts`, `notification-service.ts`, `wording-conduct.ts`. |
| 6 | When is the PDF stored? | Only when the receipt is final: signed, or the notice is past ISSUED and GIVEN_NOT_SIGNED. A withdrawn notice also gets a watermarked copy per version. Otherwise it is rendered on demand from the frozen snapshot. | Storing version 1 at issue would freeze "not yet signed" forever. Rendering from the snapshot is deterministic. | `documents/document-service.ts` (`receiptIsFinal`). |
| 7 | What does `NO_FACTS` answer? | 422 `NO_FACTS` with the month in the details. | The screen shows "No unexcused lateness in {month}". It is a valid request that cannot produce a letter, not a conflict. | `issue-service.ts` (`factsFor`). |
| 8 | Who may answer a witness request? | Anyone signed in with `notifications.own`, and only if named as the witness (else 404). | A department head sees nothing unless named; the witness card carries no reason. | `witness-service.ts`, route capability. |
| 9 | Who decides appeals? | New capability `discipline.decide`: HR for all, the Director too, narrowed in the service to notices HR issued. | Spec section 12. The access table stays the one authority. | `workforce-access.ts` row plus `decision-service.ts` check. |
| 10 | Does an appeal need a PIN? | No (spec C15). Receipt signing uses the shared `CONDUCT_SIGN` pause purpose through `attemptWithPause`: wrong tries are logged and slowed, never a lock, and the System Admin is told on the 3rd. | An appeal is a complaint, not a signature. | `appeal-service.ts`; `signer-pin.ts` takes an optional `purpose`. |
| 11 | Documents door for back end B | `registerConductDocuments(register)` hands one provider (`kind: 'conduct-notice'`) to whoever builds the door; `GET /:id/document` already serves the PDF. | The door does not exist yet. A named, tested hook is the agreed interface. | `documents/register-documents.ts`. |
| 12 | Draft preview | `POST /draft/preview` renders the same layout stamped DRAFT, number `WRN-draft`, no QR, nothing stored, no audit entry. A verbal note answers 422 `NO_LETTER`. | The manager sees the letter before signing with a PIN. | `document-service.ts` (`previewDocument`). |
| 13 | Who is audited when reading a letter? | Anyone other than the person: `conduct.document_read` in the same transaction. The person reads their own letter silently. | Spec section 11. | `document-service.ts`. |
| 14 | Nightly job | `sweepConduct` at 00:10 Nairobi (`conduct.sweep`): ends notices past their end date (no one told), tells the Director once about an appeal past 7 days, reports a silent witness. | Spec sections 6 and 11. Date work happens in Nairobi time. | `jobs/conduct-sweep-job.ts`, `workers.ts`. |
| 15 | Coexistence with the old HR screen | Two guard lines in `hr-service.ts` (create and acknowledge) refuse for a site whose attendance switch is on. Old records stay in `disciplinary_records`, read only, never counting toward the ladder. | Spec C1. No legacy table is altered. | `rollout/rollout-guard.ts`. |
| 16 | Download header | `private, no-store`, `ETag` is the file hash, `inline=1` for the phone viewer. | A letter about a person must not be cached by a shared proxy. | `documents-controller.ts`. |
