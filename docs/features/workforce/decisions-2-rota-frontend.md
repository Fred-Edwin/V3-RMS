# Slice 2 (Rota) front end: decisions made without the owner

Each row is a question the session met, what it chose, why, and what changing it would take. Nothing here blocked the work. Paper's Chapter 5 was redrawn without Publish (owner, 18 Oct 2026); the screens follow Paper and the back end, not older documents.

| # | Question | Chosen | Why | To change it |

| 1 | Is there a Save or Publish step? | No. Every set, clear, drag-fill, copy and template change is sent the moment it is made. The top bar says "Saved just now" (it ages: "Saved 3 minutes ago") and says "Not saved" with the server's words when a save fails. The only stops are the two questions the server also asks: a leave override and a reason for today or tomorrow. | Owner decision, 18 Oct 2026; the back end has no publish route. | Not planned. |
| 2 | Which week does the page open on, and what if "week starts on" is not Monday? | The week of today (Nairobi), starting Monday. | The Rules store default is Monday and the server refuses any other start; the Rules screen does not exist yet. | Read the rule when slice 7 builds Rules (`mondayOf` in `rota-dates.ts`). |
