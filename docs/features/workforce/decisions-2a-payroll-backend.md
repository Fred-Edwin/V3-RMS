# Workforce Segment 2A (Payroll back end, part 1): decisions

Written 20 Oct 2026 on branch `feat/workforce-2a-payroll-backend` (cut from `feat/workforce-segment-2`, not pushed). Each row is a question the spec or the brief left open, what was built, why, and what changing it takes. Code: `backend/src/modules/workforce/payroll/`. Spec: [design-pass-3-payroll.md](design-pass-3-payroll.md); brief: `docs/sessions/workforce-2a-payroll-backend.md`. Part 2 (publish, corrections, the bank file, Mark as paid, exports, payslip numbers) is not in this piece.

## Decisions

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | State names. The brief says CALCULATED, REVIEWED, APPROVED. | The spec's names: `WAITING`, `IN_REVIEW`, `TO_APPROVE`, `TO_PUBLISH` (part 2 adds `PUBLISHED`, `PAID` with `ALTER TYPE ... ADD VALUE`). | The approved Paper stage line and the owner's words use these; the front end shows them as they are. | Rename the enum in `payroll.prisma` and `month-stages.ts` with a migration. |
| D2 | Do ticks bump `stateVersion`? | No. Leave-out, adjustments, send, send back and approve do. | Ticking is HR's own working and must not make a second tab or the Accountant's screen stale. Anything that changes a figure or the stage must. | `review/review-service.ts` (ticks) and the other services (writes that bump). |
| D3 | What do leave-out and adjustments do to the run? | They recalculate at once and keep HR's ticks. Only the explicit "Calculate again" clears the ticks. | The totals on screen must always match the lines; ticks are HR's judgement about people, not about figures. | `calculate/calculate-service.ts` carries ticks over on lines that did not change. |
| D4 | Hours for people without a login. | Read from the approved timesheet rows directly (the hours door `getApprovedHours` answers by login only). | Casual and some staff have no login; 1A already approved their rows. | Extend the door to answer by employee, then drop `inputs-repository.ts` reads. |
| D5 | Lateness deduction. | A day counts when the person was late beyond the grace and the day is not excused; the deduction is the minutes late (not the whole day) at the hourly rate. | Matches the LATENESS rule text and 1A's excuse flag. | `engine/lines/earnings.ts`. |
| D6 | Rest day and holiday pay. | A rest day is a day with nothing scheduled. Holiday pay excludes overtime minutes already paid that day. | Wendo is open every day; no double pay. | `calculate/day-facts.ts`. |
| D7 | Order of statutory tables. | Tables not based on taxable pay are worked first, then those based on taxable pay (so PAYE sees Alpha-style relief). RATE tables limit the amount by `floorAmount` and `ceilingAmount`. | A taxable-pay table needs the others' result; floors and ceilings in the Rules shape limit the amount, not the base. | `engine/lines/statutory.ts`. |
| D8 | Rules store additions. | `STATUTORY.tables[]` gained `kind, base, reducesTaxable, isIncomeTax, employerRatePercent, reliefMonthly, floorAmount, ceilingAmount`; `LEAVE.unpaidDayDivisor` (default 30). Older stored values still parse. Logged in `slice-0-contract.md` §12. | The engine needs them; the Rules screen shows them later. | Rules defaults and parser. |
| D9 | Late approval threshold. | `LATE_APPROVAL_AFTER_DAYS` = 5 and means "more than 5 days after the month ends". The Director approves a site's timesheet month for it through 1A's approve service (`options.lateForSiteId`). | Spec P4. One tiny change to 1A rather than a copy of its approval. | The constant in `engine/payroll-constants.ts`. |
| D10 | Guard behaviour. | The old payslip run is refused with `409 PAID_IN_NEW_PAYROLL` for a covered site and month; the guard **fails closed** (an error reading the rollout answers refused). | One person is paid by one system. Wrongly refusing is recoverable; paying twice is not. | `rollout/rollout-guard.ts`, 4 lines in `payslip-service.ts`. |
| D11 | Foreign keys. | Employees, sites and users in the payroll tables are plain ids checked by services; only `PayrollMonth` has a relation (to `Company`). | No core model needed an inverse relation (the `prisma format` accident of this session came from trying). | Add relations with a migration. |
| D12 | Pay changes inside a month. | Found by halving the range; a change and a change back inside one range is missed. | Cheap and right for real data; documented rather than hidden. | Read every history row for the month instead. |
| D13 | The review "late days" figure. | It is minutes, not days (the label says so). | The engine deducts minutes. | Relabel on the front end or convert. |
| D14 | Payslip reads in the audit log. | Not audited (Segment 3, PD5 covers staff reads). | Keeps the log readable. | Add `auditRead` in `reads/`. |
| D15 | Payslip numbers (`PS-nnnn`). | Not issued yet; `document_number` is null until the first publish (part 2). The month number `PAY-YYYY-MM` is issued at approval. | Numbers belong to publish. | Part 2. |
| D16 | The Director's estimate. | A pure estimator written by the nightly job to `payroll_estimates`; it leaves out people on a daily rate (the casual sheet is separate). | One honest number for an open month; casual is paid daily from petty cash. | `estimate/estimate.ts`. |
| D17 | Blocking checks. | `NEGATIVE_NET` also covers "deductions above gross". Missing pay profile, no hours approved, and the other kinds are in `engine/review-flags.ts`. | A payslip that pays a negative amount can never be sent. | Same file. |
| D18 | Audit categories. | `PAY_RUN_PREPARE` (HR work) and `PAY_RUN_DECIDE` (Accountant, Director). Entries carry no PIN and no pay figure (proved over HTTP). | The slice 0 categories; no new ones. | `payroll-audit.ts`. |
| D19 | Access table. | `payroll.*` replaces `payrun.*`. `payrun.*` is still listed in `frontend/features/workforce/_shared/lib/capabilities.ts`: **the front end must update it.** | The table is the one source; the front end copy is not mine to edit in a back-end session. | Edit the front-end list. |
| D20 | Unpaid-month check on the attendance switch. | `attendance/switch/switch-off-check.ts` refuses switching a branch's clock off while its payroll month is unpaid (`registerPayroll()` registers it). Part 2 must extend `unpaidMonthKeys` when it adds `PUBLISHED` and `PAID`, and the migration's `payroll_months_approved_has_signature` check. | The switch must not strand an unpaid month. | `switch-off-check.ts` and a new migration. |
| D21 | Insert-only tables. | Runs, payslips, lines and snapshots refuse UPDATE and DELETE in the database. The dev teardown sets `wendo.allow_payroll_edit` for its own transaction. | A payslip is evidence. | Triggers in the migration. |
| D22 | Rules for a past month. | They cannot be saved through the Rules API (past start day), so the proof inserts the Statutory and Overtime versions unconfirmed and the Accountant confirms them through the real endpoint. | Honest proof without a back door in the product. | n/a |
