# Workforce slice 1b (Departments and heads): back-end decisions

Decisions taken during the back-end session (17 Oct 2026) where the draft contract was wrong, silent or had two reasonable answers. Each was built as the recommendation; the last column says what changing it would take. The draft contract is `feat/workforce-people:docs/features/workforce/slice-1-contract.md` (reference only). Wire names say `organization` where the code says `site`.

| # | Question | What I chose | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Which positions endpoints does Chapter 3 need? | All of O10 to O15 (list, one with holders, add, impact, edit, retire/restore), plus `positionIds` when adding or editing a department. | Chapter 3 step 6 draws the Positions tab, Add position, "Stop tracking time" and Retire. | Nothing to build; remove routes to drop any. |
| 2 | Who gets the `isDepartmentHead` / `departmentTag` claims? | Only a head (or acting head) of a department with an inventory tag (the five standard ones). A head of a department HR adds gets full Workforce authority through the head rows but no claim. | Contract §11 question 7 default; Inventory must never meet a head with no tag. | One line in `claimsFromHeadRows` (1a file `head-claims.ts`), plus Inventory agreeing to untagged heads. |
| 3 | What happens to the legacy `User.departmentId` that the old assign code kept in step with the tag? | The gateway sets it to the tagged department while the person is a head and leaves it alone when they stop. | The old code mirrored it; clearing it would wipe a person's real department now that most staff have one. | Change `setHeadClaims` in `people-account-gateway.ts` (one line each way). |
| 4 | A head row that started today is ended today. | The row is removed instead of ended. | The database refuses `ends_on < starts_on`, so an "ended" row would still count as head for today and the claim would not drop. Audit entries keep the history. | Remove `removeRow` and live with a one-day tail; or allow empty ranges in the migration. |
| 5 | Naming a new head while an acting head covers. | The acting row ends too (reason `HEAD_APPOINTED`); naming the acting head as permanent head is a promotion and is allowed. | The cover is no longer needed; refusing would force two steps. | Add an `ACTING_HEAD_EXISTS` check in `head-eligibility.ts` for `REPLACE`. |
| 6 | Acting head when the department has no head. | Allowed. | An interim head is the same act; HR should not have to invent a head first. | Add a check in `head-plan.ts`. |
| 7 | Return day of an acting head. | Must be after today; the row ends the day before it (`actingEndsOn`). The nightly reconcile drops the claim on the day it ends. | Contract §5.1. | None. |
| 8 | Who is eligible. | An `ACTIVE`, monthly employee of that department with a working login, not heading anywhere. `ONBOARDING`, `NOTICE`, casuals and switched-off logins are refused with `HEAD_NOT_ELIGIBLE` and a `reason` in `details`. | Contract §5.1; the brief says "existing active employee with a login". | Edit `ineligibleReason` in `head-eligibility.ts`. |
| 9 | Reads that need `org.write_heads` (candidates, preview). | Out of the caller's branch answers 403, not 404. | They are guarded by a write capability; the screens are only opened by someone who may assign. O1 and O2 (reads) answer 404 out of scope. | Use `assertCanReadDepartment` in `loadDepartmentForHeads`. |
| 10 | The Central Store. | Heads exist only at branches: every head endpoint on a hub department answers `409 HUB_HAS_NO_HEADS`; the overview marks the hub `headsAllowed: false` and never counts a gap there. | Contract §5.1. | Remove the hub check in `head-plan.ts`. |
| 11 | Positions are company-wide. | Writing needs `org.write` with scope `all` (HR, System Admin). Any viewer with `org.read` lists positions; the holder count is company-wide but holder names are only those the viewer may see (own branch, or the departments they head). | A position belongs to the company, not a branch. | Narrow or widen `holderScope` in `positions-service.ts`. |
| 12 | Retiring a department. | Refused while anyone who has not exited works there (`DEPARTMENT_HAS_PEOPLE`) and refused outright for the five standard departments (`DEPARTMENT_MANAGED_BY_INVENTORY`). Restoring is allowed. | Inventory retires those with its own check (open requisition sections) that this code must not bypass. | Drop the `key !== null` line in `departments-service.ts`, or call Inventory's check through its door. |
| 13 | Taking a position off a department's list, or off a position's departments. | Refused with `POSITION_HELD` if someone in that department holds it. | Otherwise a person would hold a position their department no longer offers. | Remove the check in both services. |
| 14 | Changing "clocks in" on a position. | `PATCH` needs `acknowledgedPeople` equal to the impact count (omitted means 0). The people without a personal override are switched in the same transaction, one entry with the count, one `employee.tracks_time_changed` per person. Only switching clocking **off** asks later slices for impacts. | Contract §5.1. | None. |
| 15 | Turning off "casuals allowed" on a position. | Does not move existing casuals. | A pool person already placed is not wrong, only new placements are limited. | Add a holder check in `positions-service.ts`. |
| 16 | New access capabilities. | None. `org.read`, `org.write`, `org.write_heads` already existed in the slice 0 table with the right grants (HR and System Admin all; Director read all; Branch Manager and Store Manager read unit and write heads unit; a head reads own departments). | Nothing missing. A one-line note is in the slice 0 amendment log. | Edit `workforce-access.ts`. |
| 17 | Audit actions. | Added `org.department_created/updated/retired/restored`, `org.head_changed`, `org.head_ended`, `org.head_claims_reconciled` (scheduler, channel `SYSTEM`, one entry per company per run) and `org.position_created/updated/retired/restored`, all category `PEOPLE`. Entries hold names, ids, counts and dates only. | The brief's audit rule. | Edit `audit-actions.ts`. |
| 18 | Exit and transfer of a head (1a flows). | Now go through the shared `endAllHeadRows`: rows end (or are removed if they started today), the claims are synced in the same transaction and `department.head_changed` is emitted after commit. Undoing an exit restores the role through `restoreHeadRows` the same way. A head who is on notice keeps the claim until the exit is finalised. | Brief scope item 3. | The 1a README decision 3 is now superseded. |
| 19 | The department view's people list. | Monthly staff paged (`page`, `perPage`, default 50); the casual pool is separate (up to 200) and shows the daily rate only to a viewer who may read pay. | Matches Paper's "people" and "casual pool" blocks. | Change the repository page size. |
| 20 | Who is told. | The Branch Manager, the new head and the previous head are told through the existing `told` helper (logs only until Notifications exist). | Contract §5.3. | None. |
| 21 | Files and folders. | The new code is in `people/organisation/{departments,heads,positions}`. The 1a folders `people/` (23 files) and the slice 0 `_shared/time` (15) and `rules` (13) were already over the 12-file limit and `employees-service.ts` (371 lines) over 350; this session did not touch their size. | Splitting them is a 1a clean-up, not 1b. | A small refactor session. |
