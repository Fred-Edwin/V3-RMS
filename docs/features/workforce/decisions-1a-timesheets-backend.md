# Workforce Segment 1A (Timesheets rework): back-end decisions

Written 19 Oct 2026 on branch `feat/workforce-1a-timesheets-backend`. The owner's standing rule for this session: **production-ready, not Paper-only**. Each row is a question the spec or Paper left open (or that real use needs answered), what was built, why, and what changing it takes. Code: `backend/src/modules/workforce/timesheets/` (`months/`, `approval/`, `exports/`, `overtime/`). Spec: [specs/1a-timesheets-spec.md](specs/1a-timesheets-spec.md) and [specs/decisions-1a-spec.md](specs/decisions-1a-spec.md) (cited as D1, D2 and so on). Rows marked **Beyond Paper** are additions Paper does not draw.

## Decisions

| # | Question | Choice | Why | What changing it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| X1 | Which month states are stored? | **OPEN and APPROVED are stored; TO_APPROVE is derived** from the stored state and today's Nairobi date (`months/month-state.ts`, D6). The retired `CLOSING` and `CLOSED` values stay in the Postgres enum (an enum value cannot be dropped) and are read as "not approved". | No timer can drift; nothing closes by itself (owner). | A new enum value and one function. |
| X2 | Month key and lookup | A month is the period whose **last day** falls in `YYYY-MM`. The 4a calendar-month migration already made one period per site-month; a period cut short by an old closed one still belongs to the month it ends in. | One stable key for URLs, deep links and the audit. | `periodsRepository.byMonth`. |
| X3 | What blocks "Approve the month"? (D2 refined) | **Blocks:** a held day; a day that needs a look for the clock itself (`OPEN_SHIFT`, `AUTO_CLOSED` = clock-out fix; `PHONE_CLOCK`, `UNSCHEDULED`, `CHANGED_AFTER_APPROVAL` = day to look at); overtime still pending; a request still with the manager. **Never blocks:** late, left early, absent, edited, and a request already moved to HR (it is listed). `months/needs-you.ts`, unit-tested. | Late and absence are facts payroll applies the Rules to; a request left open would be locked out the moment the month is approved. | The two reason lists in `needs-you.ts`. |
| X4 | What happens to days that only carry facts when the month is approved? | They are **approved with the month** in the same transaction (`status APPROVED`, `decidedBy` = the approver, reason "Approved with the month"), so `getApprovedHours` counts their minutes. The audit entry records how many (`daysSettledWithMonth`). | Without this a late day would stay "needs a look" forever and its hours would never reach payroll. | `settledWithMonth` and `approveWithMonth`. |
| X5 | How is approving made safe against two managers and double taps? | One transaction: `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` on the month row, re-check blocking and the figures **inside** it, settle days, snapshot, number, claim `state OPEN -> APPROVED` (`updateMany`, count 1), audit, event. Decisions (days, overtime, the head's note) take **the same lock** and refuse once it is APPROVED. Same idempotency key again answers the first result (`repeat: true`, no second entry); another key is `409 ALREADY_APPROVED`. Check order: capability and site (403/404), already approved, month ended (`MONTH_NOT_ENDED`), nothing left (`NEEDS_YOU_REMAINS` with the counts), PIN, something to approve (`NOTHING_TO_APPROVE`), figures unchanged (`CHANGED_UNDERNEATH`). | Spec S7 and D25; concurrent proof in the database flow (two simultaneous approvals: one wins). | The service. |
| X6 | Who approves a site? (D7) | `timesheet.close` is held by the **Branch Manager and Store Manager at their own site (`unit`)** and by the **Director at the site the Director belongs to (`unit`)**. HR, the Accountant and the System Admin lose it. `timesheet.reopen` is deleted. **There is no Head office site in the database yet**: the Director's own site is today the Central Store, so until a Head office site exists the Director can also approve the Central Store (the Store Manager still can). Flagged to the owner. | The table says "Director: Head office only"; the data cannot say it yet. | One site-type guard once Head office exists. |
| X7 | Who decides a site manager's own days and overtime? (**departure from the spec table**) | New capability **`manager_time.decide`** (Director, `all`). A person whose role is Branch or Store Manager is decided under it; everyone else under `timesheet.approve` / `overtime.approve`. Nobody decides their own. The approver's own hours are allowed in the month they approve and the audit entry marks it (`ownHoursIncluded`). | The spec's table gives the Director no decision rights, but a Branch Manager's own overtime or open shift would then block approving the month forever (HR can no longer decide it). | Drop the capability and decide where such an item goes. |
| X8 | HR and the System Admin still answer requests | New capability **`time_request.answer`** (Branch/Store Manager `unit`, HR `all`, System Admin `all`), split from `timesheet.approve`. | The spec keeps escalation to HR after 3 days; HR must still answer, but may not approve days or overtime. | Grants in the access table. |
| X9 | Department head | Loses `timesheet.read`, `timesheet.approve` and `overtime.approve` (D7); gains **`overtime.note`** (`dept`). Heads keep their department's **lateness report and a day's clock history** on the Today board: those two reads fall back to `today.read` (`attendanceReadCapability`). | The head decides nothing; the Today board is slice 3's and must not break. | The helper. |
| X10 | Head's note (D24) | One line, 2 to 140 characters, once, only while the item is pending and the month is not approved, for an item of the head's own department; versioned; audited (`timesheet.overtime_noted`); shown as evidence, never a decision. | Review default. | `overtime-note-service.ts`. |
| X11 | Overtime decision rules (D3, D4) | `APPROVE_ALL`, `APPROVE_PART` or `DECLINE`; part moves in 5-minute steps from 5 to the extra minus 5 (an item of under 10 minutes cannot be split); a reason of **at least 5 characters** for part or none (optional for all), at most 280; the manager's **own PIN**; the **version** the screen showed. Stores `authorisedMinutes` (the service-tail part stays authorised either way), status `APPROVED_PART` (new) and `decisionReason`. The answer **can be changed until the month is approved** (each answer is its own audit entry). The built rule `OVERTIME_NEEDS_MANAGER` is gone; approving beyond the weekly cap or branch budget still needs a reason (`REASON_REQUIRED_OVER_LIMIT`). | Spec S6. | `overtime-plan.ts` (`decisionOutcome`). |
| X12 | Versions and "two people at once" (D25) | `overtime_items.version` (integer) is bumped by every write, including the system's own refresh when the minutes change; a stale version answers `409 CHANGED_UNDERNEATH`. The month approval compares the dialog's totals instead of a version. Days carry no version (their decision rule is "status must still be NEEDS_LOOK or HELD"). | Smallest thing that makes a double tap and a second manager safe. | A `version` on days. |
| X13 | Overtime evidence | **Counts only, never takings** (spec). The built day read (T5) used to carry the takings of the extra-time orders; that is removed (`valueOfOrders` deleted). The card gives planned and worked times, the reason from the shift-end prompt, orders open at the end (orders only: open tickets are not stored), orders taken and tickets cleared, the head's note, and `colleagues { team, rostered, present }` (the roster of the person's department that day and who clocked in). `askedInAdvance` is always `false`: no advance-request feature exists. "Colleagues free" from Paper is not derivable without the department roster; rostered and present are given instead. | The brief, and what the data can honestly say. | Store the open tickets; add the roster read. |
| X14 | The frozen snapshot | At approval the person rows (totals, "decided by" lines as codes with names), the month totals and the rules used are stored as JSON (`approved_snapshot`) with `document_hash = sha256` of a key-sorted serialisation. The **PDF and the Excel's first sheets of an approved month are built from the snapshot**, so they never change; the Excel's daily sheet and a range or one-person extract come from the live day rows, which an approved month no longer changes (refresh and every write refuse an APPROVED month). | Spec S7: "the PDF and the Excel of an approved month never change". | `approval/snapshot.ts`. |
| X15 | Document number (D12) | `TSH-` plus 4 digits from the database sequence `timesheet_document_seq`, issued **at approval**; gaps allowed. The old `report_number` column (issued at creation) and `cutoff_at` are retired and left in place (cutoff now stores the instant the month can first be approved). The migration numbers old approved months by month order. | A number with nothing signed under it would mislead. | The sequence. |
| X16 | Late | "Late, recorded" counts **any lateness** (`minutesLate > 0`, the spec's reading of its Paper note 4), per day; payroll applies the Rules (grace, policy). Absence counts days with an `ABSENT` reason. Leave days are 0 until slice 5. | Spec section 17, item 4. | `people-rows.ts`. |
| X17 | Months list | 5 a page with a total; the current month is created on first read (as built); figures by aggregates and a handful of small reads, never by loading every day; the open month reports hours "so far" (D18: days before today plus today's finished days). | The page must stay quick with 200 people. | `months-repository.ts`. |
| X18 | People table | Built in memory from one load of the month (at most about 200 people by 31 days), filtered, sorted and **paged on the server** (50 a page, 5 to 100). Default sort department then name A to Z, so the table, the PDF and the Excel agree; sortable by person, worked, overtime, late. Chip counts follow the range and department, not the search. | Table convention (UI_BUILD_RULES 4a). | Move to SQL aggregates if a site passes about 500 people. |
| X19 | Routes | Renamed to `/workforce/timesheets/months/...` (list, month, people, a person's drawer, a person's days, approve, exports). The built period list/read/close/reopen/sign/report routes are **removed with no compatibility layer**; the front-end session replaces them. The months reads take `site` (the spec's name), the older overtime and request lists keep `siteId`. | One feature, one set of reads; the front end is rebuilt next. | n/a |
| X20 | Exports: libraries | `pdfkit` was already in the repository (the old reports) and draws the PDF. **Added `exceljs` 4.4.0** (Excel with real numbers, live `SUM` formulas, frozen header and first column, filters) and **`qrcode` 1.5.4** with `@types/qrcode` (the QR is drawn as vector squares). Nothing in the repository wrote .xlsx or a QR. | Brief: reuse first, log any addition. | Swap the renderer behind `renderXlsx`. |
| X21 | Exports: fonts | The PDF uses the standard Times fonts (Times-Roman, Bold, Italic: metric-compatible with Times New Roman, present in every viewer, not embedded). **Update 19 Oct 2026 (owner):** the signature is now drawn in **Alex Brush** (`backend/assets/fonts/`, SIL OFL licence beside it, first and second name only; the Dockerfile copies the folder; if the file is missing the export falls back to Times-Italic). **Tinos is still not embedded.** | Spec D14 asked for embedded Tinos and Alex Brush. | Add the Tinos file and `doc.registerFont` in `pdf-render.ts`. |
| X22 | Exports: limits and the background job | Synchronous only. **More than 400 people: 413 `EXPORT_TOO_LARGE`**; **12 exports per person per 10 minutes: 429 `EXPORT_RATE_LIMIT`**; a file that takes over 20 s: 504 `EXPORT_TOO_SLOW`. **No background job and no `202 {jobId}`** (the spec offered one over 200 people): a month of 200 people renders in well under a second here. | A job queue and a Notifications row for a case that does not occur yet would be dead code. | A queue job when a site passes about 400 people. |
| X23 | Exports: audit and repeats | Table `timesheet_exports` (who, format, scope, range, document number, idempotency key). One audit entry (`timesheet.exported`: who, format, month, range, person for an extract, document number or `draft`) per key; a repeat of the same key makes the file again and writes no second entry. The file is not stored. **Never any pay figure**: a test scans the Excel for money words. | Spec D32; double taps and lost responses. | n/a |
| X24 | Exports: who and what | `timesheet.export`: Branch Manager and Store Manager (own site), HR and the Accountant (all). The Director and the System Admin read but do not export (D9). Draft: "DRAFT · NOT APPROVED" stamp, `TSH-draft`, a light diagonal watermark, an unsigned block, no QR. Approved: stamp, number, signature, QR. A range or one-person extract of an approved month keeps the approved stamp and adds "Extract: ..." (D13). The response is the file with `Content-Disposition` and `X-Document-Number`. | Spec S3 and section 10. | n/a |
| X25 | Page plan (D16) | Departments A to Z, whole departments, 20 people on page 1 and 26 on later pages, never split unless a department alone exceeds 26 ("BARISTA, continued" with the header repeated); **plus a height rule** (24 lines on page 1, 32 later, a line being a person or a department band) so many tiny departments cannot overflow a page; the rules box and signature (158 pt tall) sit on the last page only when they fit, else a last page of their own. Paper's sample (9, 8, 5, 9) makes 2 pages. Pure and unit-tested. | Spec D16 plus a real-use edge. | Constants in `export-plan.ts`. |
| X26 | QR payload (D15) | `<FRONTEND_ORIGIN>/verify/tsh/TSH-0009?h=<first 10 hex of the hash>`. **The verify page (Chapter 17) is not built**: the QR leads nowhere until it is. | Spec D15; dependency noted. | n/a |
| X27 | The sweep | Every 10 minutes: brings each switched branch's current month up to date, **refreshes every month that is not approved** (so a manager sees the last days complete after the month ends), and escalates requests after 3 days. It closes nothing. An approved month is never touched. | Spec section 7. Cost: a month waiting for approval is re-read every 10 minutes; each refresh writes only what changed. | Limit the refresh to months ended less than N days ago. |
| X28 | Clock-fix door (spec 4) | The hook answers only for an **approved** month: `409 PERIOD_APPROVED`, "This month is approved. Ask HR to correct the payslip." It applies to everyone, HR included. Before approval the Branch Manager fixes alone; HR keeps `time.fix` (D10). The same code and wording are used for decisions and overtime on an approved month. | Spec D10 and D29. | `period-lock.ts`. |
| X29 | Requests on an approved month (D29) | A new request about a day in an approved month is stored and goes straight to HR. HR cannot apply a clock correction on it (the door refuses); the correction is the payslip's (Segment 2). | Spec D29. | `requests-service.ts`. |
| X30 | `getApprovedHours` | Reads **APPROVED months only**: approved days' minutes and the authorised overtime minutes (approved, approved in part, the automatic part of a declined item, and service-tail items). Before approval it returns 0. Segment 2 extends this door. | Brief point 1. | `approved-repository.ts`. |
| X31 | Late clock events after approval | The spec asks for them to be stored and flagged `AFTER_APPROVAL`. Nothing flags them: the refresh never touches an approved month, so approved hours cannot change, and **Segment 2's read lists clock events newer than `approved_at`** for HR. | The flag would need a write into slice 3's tables. | Add the read in Segment 2. |
| X32 | Notifications | HR and the Accountant are told on approval (`timesheet.month_approved`, site and month, never a figure); the person is told an overtime answer (`overtime.decided` with minutes granted, minutes asked and the reason for part or none only). Delivery is still the log-only notifier; the wording table is the front end's. | Spec D27, D28. | n/a |
| X33 | Migration | Two migrations, additive, legacy tables untouched: `20261019140000_workforce_month_approval` (new enum values, columns, `timesheet_exports`, the sequence; written with `IF NOT EXISTS` so a repeat is harmless) and `20261019140100_workforce_month_approval_data` (a new enum value cannot be used in the transaction that adds it, so the data step is its own file): CLOSED, signed or not -> APPROVED with the signer or closer, the time, a document number in month order and a note that it predates the change; CLOSING -> OPEN. Proved on a copy in the old shape: nothing lost, every other table identical, a second run changes nothing and spends no number (`scripts/proof/timesheets-approval-migration-proof.sh`). | Brief point 8. | n/a |
| X34 | `timesheetCutoff` | Not read by anything (`period-window.ts` takes only `timesheetPeriod`); the field stays parseable. | Brief point 9, D30. | Delete the field in 1B. |
| X35 | Per-day "edited by" | `editedById` is the person recorded on the day's clock correction when there is one; otherwise it is null though the day reads "Edited". | The day model carries only one author field. | Read the correction rows. |
| X36 | Part weeks | Unchanged: the weekly overtime cap still reads the whole Monday to Sunday week (4a, M5). | Out of scope. | n/a |

## Error codes the front end maps

`NO_TIMESHEET` (404), `RANGE_OUTSIDE_MONTH` (400), `MONTH_NOT_ENDED` (409, `approvableFrom`), `NEEDS_YOU_REMAINS` (409, the counts), `CHANGED_UNDERNEATH` (409), `ALREADY_APPROVED` (409), `NOTHING_TO_APPROVE` (409), `PIN_NOT_SET` (409), `INVALID_PIN` (401), `PART_OUT_OF_RANGE` (409), `REASON_REQUIRED_OVER_LIMIT` (409), `NOTHING_TO_DECIDE` (409), `PERIOD_APPROVED` (409), `ALREADY_NOTED` and `NOT_PENDING` (409), `EXPORT_TOO_LARGE` (413), `EXPORT_RATE_LIMIT` (429), `EXPORT_TOO_SLOW` (504).
