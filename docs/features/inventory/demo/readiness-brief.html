Terms the client will hear

Buy unit and usage unit
The unit an item is bought in (a 25 kg bag) and the unit it is used in (kilograms). The conversion links them.
Requisition
A department’s request for stock, filled by its head and approved by the Branch Manager.
Dispatch
Stock sent from the Central Store to a branch, signed per department.
Blind count
A stock count where the counter cannot see the expected quantity, so the number is independent.
Ledger
The record of every stock movement. Stock is never edited directly.
PIN sign
A four-digit code that puts a name and a time on a step such as a receipt or dispatch.
