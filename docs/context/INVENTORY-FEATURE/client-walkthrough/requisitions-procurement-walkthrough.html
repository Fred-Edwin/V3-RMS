Requesting Supplies

Department Head → Branch Manager

Any department — Kitchen, Bar, Pastry, and so on — can be running low on something mid-shift. Instead of a phone call or a paper note, the department head raises a request on their phone, and the Branch Manager reviews it before it's sent anywhere.

Fulfilling & Receiving

Store Manager → Department Head

Once a request is approved, it lands with the Central Store to be packed and sent. The department checks off what actually arrives — if something's short, that's flagged automatically rather than quietly absorbed.

Workflow 3 of 5

Buying From the Market

Department Head → Branch Manager

Market produce doesn't come from the Central Store, so it works differently. A department head never goes to the market themselves — they request what they need, the Branch Manager consolidates every department's requests and sends someone to buy it, then each department confirms what actually landed on their shelf.

1
Department Head · Mobile

Requests market items ahead of time

For produce a department knows it'll need. This request joins one shared order with whatever other departments have also requested — so the branch makes one trip, not several.

Screen placeholder
Mobile
2
Branch Manager · Desktop

Reviews the combined order and approves it

Sees every department's requests grouped together in one draft, can adjust any line, then approves the whole order to be bought.

Screen placeholder
Draft Drawer · Desktop
3
Delivery person

Buys the order and brings it back

The approved order goes out to be bought — not by the department head or the Branch Manager. No screen here; this step happens off-system, at the market.

4
Branch Manager · Desktop

Logs what actually came back

Once the order is delivered, the Branch Manager records the real quantity bought and price paid for each item against what was originally requested.

Screen placeholder
Reconciling Drawer · Desktop
5
Department Head · Mobile

Confirms their portion into the department's stock

Each department only sees and confirms the items they asked for — not the whole branch-wide order. Confirming logs the quantity straight into that department's stock sheet.

Matches

Confirmed received, no further action.

Doesn't match

Variance is shown clearly against what was originally requested.
