Inventory Management — How It Works End to End

From raw ingredients at the Central Kitchen to a customer being served at a branch

The Big Picture
SUPPLIERS │ deliver raw ingredients ▼ CENTRAL KITCHEN ──── stores & prepares ──► branches ┌──────┴──────┐ ▼ ▼ BRANCH A BRANCH B receives receives serves serves └──────┬──────┘ ▼ customers get their orders Every box tracked. Every arrow a recorded event.
Step 1 — Supplier Delivers to Central Kitchen
Store Manager logs the delivery
┌─────────────────────────────┐ │ Log Incoming Delivery │ │ Item: Chicken Breast │ │ Quantity: 50 kg │ │ Supplier: Kamau Farms │ │ Date: Today │ │ [ Confirm Receipt ] │ └─────────────────────────────┘

System now knows: Central Kitchen has 50 kg chicken.

Step 2 — Conversion Table (set once by management)
System calculates portions automatically
Chicken Burger = 0.15 kg → 333 portions Chicken Pizza = 0.20 kg → 250 portions Chicken Rice Bowl = 0.18 kg → 277 portions (from 50 kg of chicken)

Store Manager sees how many portions are available at any moment. No manual calculation.

Step 3 — Branch Submits Morning Requisition
Branch Manager requests what they need
┌──────────────────────────────────┐ │ Morning Requisition — Kingz │ │ Item Requested │ │ Chicken Burger 40 portions │ │ Chicken Pizza 20 portions │ │ Beef Stew 30 portions │ │ Cappuccino 50 portions │ │ Notes: school group at lunch │ │ [ Send Requisition ] │ └──────────────────────────────────┘
Step 4 — Central Kitchen Reviews & Dispatches
Store Manager sees all branches in one view
Kingz 07:15 AM Pending [Review] Town 07:22 AM Pending [Review]

System checks stock and flags what cannot be fully fulfilled:

Item Asked Can Fill Status Chicken Burger 40 40 ✓ Full Beef Stew 30 15 ⚠ Partial [ Approve & Dispatch ]

On approval: stock deducted, dispatch recorded, branch notified.

Step 5 — Branch Receives & Confirms Delivery
Receiving person checks against what was sent
Item Sent Received Chicken Burger 40 40 ✓ Beef Stew 15 12 ⚠ short [ Confirm Receipt ]

Discrepancy flagged to both Branch Manager and Store Manager. Does not disappear quietly.

Beef Stew is short 3 portions. Mark it unavailable on the menu? [ Yes, hide it ] [ No, keep it ]
Step 6 — Branch Opens. Stock Depletes Automatically.
Every completed order reduces branch stock — no manual action
Branch Stock — Kingz (Live) Chicken Burger ████████████░░ 38/40 Beef Stew ████████░░░░░░ 12/15 Cappuccino █████████████░ 48/50

Waiters see nothing different. System tracks silently in the background.

Step 7 — Low Stock Alert & Auto-Hide
System alerts before stock runs out
⚠ Low Stock Alert — Kingz Branch Chicken Burger is down to 4 portions. Submit mid-day requisition or hide item.

When stock hits zero → item automatically hidden from menu. Waiters cannot take orders for it.

Step 8 — End of Day Stocktake
Manager counts remaining portions, enters actual numbers
Item Expected Actual Variance Chicken Burger 2 2 ✓ 0 Beef Stew 4 2 ✗ -2 ⚠ Cappuccino 3 3 ✓ 0 2 Beef Stew portions unaccounted for. Note: [ ] [ Submit Count ]

Every gap is recorded. Patterns across days become visible to management.

Step 9 — Management Dashboard
Director & Store Manager see everything, always
Branch Requisitions Discrepancies Shrinkage Kingz Received ✓ 1 flagged 3 portions Town Received ✓ 0 flagged 0 portions Central Kitchen Raw Stock: Chicken Breast ████████░░ 18 kg Coffee Beans ██░░░░░░░░ 4.2 kg ⚠ reorder
The Full Loop — Summary
SUPPLIER │ delivers raw ingredients ▼ CENTRAL KITCHEN │ logs receipt → system knows stock │ branch submits requisition │ system checks availability │ store manager approves & dispatches ▼ BRANCH │ receives & confirms delivery │ discrepancy → immediately flagged │ service starts → stock depletes automatically │ low stock → manager alerted │ zero stock → item hidden from menu │ end of day → physical count entered ▼ REPORTS (Director / Store Manager) - Ordered vs fulfilled - Dispatched vs received - Expected vs actually consumed - Where losses are happening - Which branch, item, day
What This Prevents
Problem How the System Stops It
Items missing between CK and branch Dispatch vs received flags every gap
Orders taken for unavailable items Auto-hide when stock hits zero
Stock vanishing mid-shift Daily stocktake creates a paper trail
Management blind to losses Every variance recorded and visible
Branches over- or under-ordering Historical data informs future requests
CK running out of raw ingredients Live stock levels + low-stock alerts

No gap in the chain is invisible. Every handover is recorded. Every discrepancy is surfaced. The Director can sit anywhere and know exactly what is happening at every branch.
