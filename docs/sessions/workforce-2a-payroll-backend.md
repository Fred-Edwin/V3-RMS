### Scope (part 1)
1. **Data (additive migration, proved on a copy of the lane database):** payroll months per company (status: WAITING, CALCULATED, REVIEWED, APPROVED; PUBLISHED and PAID are added by part 2, leave a clean extension point), the sites each month includes and their timesheet approvals, payslips as frozen computed rows with each line's working (version 1; versioning mechanics for corrections belong to part 2 but the column exists), review lines (blocking and ticked), HR adjustments, calculation runs with a hash of the inputs. **Money is stored as integer cents or a decimal type per the existing convention; check `DATA_MODEL.md` and follow it.**
2. **The table-driven calculation engine** exactly as the spec: no tax name, rate or band in code; inputs from `getApprovedHours`, the pay profile, authorised overtime minutes and multipliers, lateness and absence deductions (minutes at the hourly rate, capped by the rule's percentage, P12), holiday pay, leave days as 0 until Leave is built, casual pay as a memo line only; order and rounding per the spec; "each line shows its working"; sanity checks that block or warn; refuses to calculate if the Statutory tables or pay rates are not confirmed (`isPayConfirmed`), and says why. Pure functions in their own folder with thorough unit tests (including a part-month joiner and leaver, a person with no pay profile or no bank account, a negative net, overtime part-approved, and the Nairobi versus UTC month boundary), plus the three time-zone runs.
3. **The month and stages up to Approved:** list months (including the open month), read a month (stage line, KPIs, sites and their approval status, the tabs' data: timesheets, rules applied, tax and deductions, casual memo, totals, payslips list), calculate (idempotent; recalculating while CALCULATED or REVIEWED is allowed and re-opens review), **HR review** (tick lines, adjustments, send-to-Accountant), **the Accountant approves with a PIN** (the preparer can never approve; HR cannot approve for a site; wrong, missing or locked-out PIN handled like Rules), **send back to HR with a reason** (P18), the late-approver route for the Director (P4), the waiting banner for unconfirmed tax tables. Version or lock every write so two people cannot act on the same month at once.
4. **The rollout guard (the most important production rule):** a Payroll month includes only sites whose attendance clock is on and that have approved their month; each person is paid by exactly one system. Add the small additive guard to the legacy payslip code that refuses to save, publish or revert a payslip for any site and month the new Payroll covers, exactly as the spec says; legacy tables untouched; the other sites keep the old run. Test both directions (a covered site refused in the old code; an uncovered site unchanged).
5. **The Director's estimate** (P28): gross only, from pay profiles and authorised overtime, refreshed nightly (a sweep job; run it directly if Redis is down), never exported, states for a site not on the new system.
6. **Access:** add capabilities to the access table (one table, role by capability; log each in `slice-0-contract.md` §12): Accountant approves and confirms; HR prepares, reviews; the Director and System Admin read; Branch Managers see no payroll; the preparer-is-not-approver rule enforced in the service. Audit events for every write (never pay figures), events through the Workforce events door, notifications log-only.
**Out:** publish, corrections, Mark as paid and the lock, the bank file, PDF and Excel exports (part 2); payslip phone endpoints and casual pay recording (Segment 3); any frontend; changing legacy tables.
