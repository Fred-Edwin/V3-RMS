## Choices made that must go in the decisions file
Use the spec's state names (WAITING, IN_REVIEW, TO_APPROVE, TO_PUBLISH) not the brief's CALCULATED/REVIEWED/APPROVED; ticks do not bump `stateVersion` (leave-out, adjustments, send, approve do); leave-out and adjustments recalculate straight away and keep HR's ticks (only the explicit "Calculate again" clears them); hours for people without a login are read from the approved rows (the hours door answers by login); lateness counts a day when beyond grace and not excused, deducting its minutes late; rest day = nothing scheduled that day; holiday pay excludes overtime minutes already paid that day; statutory tables not based on taxable pay are worked first, then taxable-pay ones; RATE floor/ceiling limit the amount; LATE_APPROVAL_AFTER_DAYS is "more than 5"; the guard fails closed; employees/sites/users in payroll tables are plain ids (only `PayrollMonth` has a relation, to `Company`); pay change segments found by halving (a change and change back inside one range is missed); the review "late days" figure is minutes not days; payslip reads are not audited (Segment 3, PD5); no `PS-` sequence yet (part 2); estimator excludes daily-rate people; NEGATIVE_NET also covers "deductions above gross"; the audit categories used are PAY_RUN_PREPARE and PAY_RUN_DECIDE; `payrun.*` is still listed in `frontend/features/workforce/_shared/lib/capabilities.ts` (front end must update).
