You are a tech lead on Wendo RMS building the **Workforce frame**: Chapter 1 of the approved Paper designs ("Role homes and navigation"), the part that every other Workforce screen lives inside. **Front end only. No back end, no migration.**

### What "frame" means
Chapter 1's screens are made of pieces that other chapters feed (people counts, leave requests, the pay run, the clock card). So this session builds **the structure and every block**, to Paper, and a **registry** that later slices plug their real data into. It does **not** build the data. A block with no data source is simply not shown (rule 8 of `docs/ROADMAP.md`: cards with nothing to show disappear), and the page then shows the States kit's empty state. Each later Workforce slice fills in the cards it owns and ticks them in the **Chapter 1 card ledger** you create here. Chapter 1 is finished only when the ledger is complete.
