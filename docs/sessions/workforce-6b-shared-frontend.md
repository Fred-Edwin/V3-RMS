### Facts from the back-end reports you must honour
- **The verify page is public.** The QR on every document opens `${FRONTEND_ORIGIN}/verify/<kind>/<number>?h=<10 characters>` and the page must work **with no sign-in**: make the Next.js route and `middleware.ts` / `route-access.ts` allow `/verify/*` for anyone, and prove it in a private window. It calls the public API `GET /api/v1/verify/:kind/:number?h=` (never cached; rate limited; there are seven outcomes: genuine, replaced, voided, unknown, malformed, hash mismatch, rate limited). **It shows role and date only, never a name, a figure or a head count** (owner decision, 20 Oct 2026; this differs from Paper Chapter 17 on purpose, and the "total differs" message is not built because it would leak). A signed-in person gets the richer `GET /workforce/documents/verify/:kind/:number?h=` and may report a problem (`POST .../report { h, idempotencyKey }`).
- **Documents index:** `GET /workforce/documents` takes `q, type, siteId, from, to, sort, page, pageSize`; **`pageSize` must be 25, 50 or 100 (anything else is a 400)** and unknown query keys are a 400. Only the types the person may read are listed or counted. Use the table convention (search and filters first, numbered pager, no infinite scroll). **`openHref` is an API route, and for payroll, timesheet and P9 documents it is a POST route:** open them with the right method, via `POST /workforce/documents/:number/open` (it writes `document.opened`).
- **Audit log:** `GET /workforce/audit/entries` with Area and Event filters, actor, subject, date range, flagged, search, sort and a numbered pager (exact counts to 50,000, then `totalIsExact: false`: say "more than 50,000"); detail with entry number and hash; facets; `/actors?q=` needs `q`; `/viewers` is for the Director. **CSV export** is capped at 50,000 rows, escaped, and one `audit.exported` entry is written first. **"Check the chain"** is `POST /workforce/audit/chain/check { fromSeq, toSeq }`: `429 CHECK_TOO_SOON` (once a minute), a 400 if the slice is over the cap, and a break writes its own entry; show these states plainly. The Director reads everything, read only; role visibility per Area comes from the API, never from role names in the page.
- **Activity on my record** (`GET /workforce/audit/me`, `/me/:id`, `POST /me/:id/answer { answer, idempotencyKey }`): "This wasn't me" calls the front end A security flow; keep one flow, do not rebuild it.
- **The Director's payslip-opens digest:** `GET /workforce/payslips/opens?from&to` (a range). It shows who opened what and when, **never amounts**.
- **Chain check and verify have real rate limits** (a chain check once a minute, 30 verifies a minute): do not hammer them in your own testing; one run, then wait.
- Not built on the back end: a "Weekly rota" document (excluded by the spec), and nothing for payslips shown to the Director in the documents index (the access table does not allow it; follow the API).
- The documents door is wired for Payroll and the other built issuers by `reconcileDocuments`. **Leave slips (LVE) and conduct notices (WRN) may not appear in the index yet** because a small separate session still connects them (`docs/sessions/workforce-docs-door-wiring.md`); build for them, and do not treat their absence in your lane as your bug.
