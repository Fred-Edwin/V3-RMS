## A. Gaps between Paper and the contract

### A1. Arrival (blocks D7, D12 and the 2-hour timer)
1. **"Arrived" has no source.** D7 says "Arrived · Your delivery is here"; D12 has a tracker line "Arrived · 3:28 pm", separate from "Left the Central Store 3:05 pm". The contract has only `ON_THE_WAY` (set at the final sign) and "over 2 hours since arrival", but no endpoint or field says when it arrived. Options: (a) arrival is the first time anyone from the department opens the delivery (the back end stamps `arrivedAt` on the first V2 read); (b) a "It has arrived" button (not drawn); (c) arrival is the sign time. **Need decided. Default: (a), with `arrivedAt` returned by V1, V2 and V6. Before it is stamped the card reads "On the way" in the same style (see B13).** Whatever you pick changes the 2-hour rule (counted from `arrivedAt`, or from the sign time).
2. Paper times disagree: D5 signs at 3:03, D6 says "went out at 3:05", D7 "Left the Central Store 3:05", D12 "Packed and signed 3:03". In the system signing is the moment stock leaves. **Default: show `signedAt` everywhere.**

### A2. Packing (D1 to D6)
3. **D1, department order and "Start here".** The contract does not order departments. **Default: the Block 1 departments table order; "Start here" is on the first department that is not fully ticked.** The oldest branch card is open, the others collapsed (as drawn). The "Waiting 29 min" chip turns amber at some threshold (29 min amber, 13 min grey in Paper). **Need the threshold. Default: amber from 20 minutes.** The wait counts from approval and the card updates each minute without a refetch.
4. **D2, "Tap a number to change it".** Paper draws a number box on every line but only draws the stepper sheet (D3) for a short line. **Default: tapping the number on any line opens the same stepper sheet.** Questions: (a) may the Attendant send more than asked? Default no, the maximum is the smaller of asked and in store. (b) may they send 0? Default yes ("Send none" on the button; the line still ticks), which counts as a short line. (c) a line with 0 in store: the sheet opens at 0 and the line is short.
5. **D2, the unit.** Paper shows the unit only on some lines ("Asked 4 bags", but "Asked 22", "Asked 3"). **Default: show the catalog unit after both numbers unless the item name already carries it ("(crate)", "(kg)").**
6. **D2, categories.** Paper draws one heading per category ("PREP KITCHEN", "DRY ITEMS"). Block 1's two-level paths use the same default: one heading, joined with " · ".
7. **D2 to D4, going from department to department.** "Done with Kitchen" goes to the next department that is not fully ticked; when none is left it goes to D4. D4 also opens from D1 on a requisition that is fully ticked but not yet signed. Tapping a department on D4 reopens it with the ticks kept. **Default as described.**
8. **D4, not everything is packed, and the left-out rule (frozen contract: the Attendant may leave a department out).** Paper draws only the all-packed state. 10. **D5, the carrier list.** The Attendant needs the active carriers to choose from, but the contract gives `carriers.read` only to Director, Accountant and Branch Manager, and `carriers.manage` to the Store Manager and System Admin. **Need: the Attendant gets read of active carriers, or P4 returns the active list.** Default: P4 returns it. 11. **D5, who pre-fills "Packed by" and "Signed by".** Both show the title "Store Attendant" (read only). When the Store Manager packs, it shows "Store Manager". Titles, not names, as drawn.
12. **P5, what can go wrong that has no error code.** Two requisitions can pack the last of one item at the same time. The contract lists only `NOT_ALL_PACKED`, `INVALID_PIN`, `CARRIER_INACTIVE`, `NOTHING_TO_SEND`. **Need: the code and wording when stock changed under the Attendant** (`INSUFFICIENT_STOCK`? the ledger door may refuse a negative). ### A3. Receiving (D7 to D12)
