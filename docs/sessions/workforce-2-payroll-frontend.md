### Facts from the back-end reports you must honour
- `capabilities.ts` and the permission hook still list `payrun.*`: change them to `payroll.*` (no new capabilities were needed); drop anything the back end no longer has.
- The month read has new fields: `lockedAt`, `publishedAt`, `paidAt`, `canPay`, `canCorrect`. The payslips list has `status=corrected` and counts. State names are WAITING, IN_REVIEW, TO_APPROVE, TO_PUBLISH, PUBLISHED, PAID (the spec's, not older names).
- Every POST takes an `idempotencyKey`; month writes take the month's `stateVersion`; a correction takes `expectedVersion`. A stale write answers 409 `STATE_STALE`-style codes: show them in words and offer to reload.
- A wrong PIN is `403 PIN_INVALID`, not 401 (the apps read 401 as signed out); five wrong tries in ten minutes lock signing (`429 PIN_LOCKED`).
- **Mark as paid needs the `X-Bank-File-Hash` header from the bank-file download**: keep it from the download response and send it back as `bankFileHash`. `ALREADY_PAID` and `MONTH_LOCKED` are separate codes. After Paid every write is refused: show the lock plainly.
- The bank file and the exports are file downloads (`Content-Disposition`, document numbers in headers): handle them as downloads, never as JSON. Exports above 400 people answer 413; 12 per person per 10 minutes then 429; over 30 seconds 504.
- A person left out of a month has no payslip and is paid next month (not blocked).
- The review "late days" figure is minutes: label it as minutes.
- The Casual pay tab now reads the approved casual sheets (Segment 3 back end).
- The A4 payslip in the zip is a plain version until Segment 3's stored one replaces it.
