### What changes (read `docs/features/workforce/segment-1-review.md` first)
1. **Month status machine per site.** OPEN, TO_APPROVE (month has ended or the manager is ready), APPROVED (locked, with payroll). Remove the automatic 18:00 close, **Close early**, **HR reopen** and the report PIN signature as built; replace with **Approve the month**: one action by the site's manager with a PIN, blocked until every exception is decided (default; the spec may refine), recording who, when and a snapshot of approved minutes. Payroll stages after APPROVED (Calculated to Paid) belong to Segment 2: leave a clean extension point and `getApprovedHours` returning approved minutes for APPROVED months only.
2. **Approvers.** Each site's manager approves their own site: Branch Manager; Store Manager for the Central Store; Director for Head office. **HR cannot approve for a site.** Accountant and System Admin read only. Update `_shared/workforce-access.ts`, its test and `slice-0-contract.md` §12 (code wins; log amendments). A **department head approves nothing**: remove the head's overtime-allowance decision path; a head may leave a one-line **note** on an overtime item (evidence, never a decision).
3. **Overtime decision** becomes approve **all, part or none** (minutes), with a reason (required for part or none) and the manager's PIN. Store minutes authorised; payroll decides pay. Keep the evidence (counts only; never takings) and limits.
4. **Clock-fix rule** (attendance fix door): before the month is approved the Branch Manager (own site) fixes alone; after approval the door refuses with a clear code and message ("the month is approved; ask HR to correct the payslip"). HR's reopen path is gone. Update `attendance/fixes/period-lock.ts`, `register-timesheets.ts` and their tests.
5. **Months list and the month read** for the page: months for a site newest first including the **open month with "so far"** (hours and people to date), status per month, and a month read with KPIs (hours worked versus plan, overtime recorded and approved, late recorded, "needs you before approving" with its parts), the people table (search, department filter, date range with week quick picks, needs-a-look and late filters, 50 per page, pager), and a person's month (at a glance, days that need a look, last 7 days, every day for the full-month page).
6. **Exports.** The timesheet as **PDF** (A4 landscape, grouped by department then A to Z, 20 people on page 1, a department never split across pages, signed document style with QR and document number TSH-n once approved, stamped "Draft, not approved" before approval) and as **Excel** (one row per person, real numbers, notes which signed document it matches). Reuse the repository's existing PDF and spreadsheet approach if one exists (check `backend/package.json` and the Inventory documents first); add a dependency only if nothing fits, and log it. Replace the CSV. Exports are audited (who, when, which month), never contain pay figures, and are limited in size and time.
7. **Removed from the sweep**: the auto-close step. Keep the assemble step (days refresh) and request escalation to HR after 3 days. Report a problem goes to the Branch Manager; after the month is approved the answer is "tell HR".
8. **Data migration** (additive; legacy tables untouched; proved on a copy of the lane database): map existing periods (OPEN, CLOSING, CLOSED, signed) onto the new statuses without losing a day, item or request; a previously CLOSED or signed period becomes APPROVED with its approver, time and a note that it predates the change. Second run changes nothing.
9. **Rules store**: stop using `timesheetCutoff` (leave the field parseable); `timesheetPeriod` stays `CALENDAR_MONTH`.
