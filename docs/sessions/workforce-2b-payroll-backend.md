### Scope (part 2)
1. **Publish** (HR, after the Accountant approved): creates the frozen payslips visible to staff with PS-n numbers from a database sequence issued at publish; idempotent; staff are told (no figures); the month becomes PUBLISHED. Note the legacy guard stays in force.
2. **Corrections until Paid** (HR or the Accountant): one payslip, a correction kind with a live preview (before and after), a reason and PIN; the payslip becomes **version 2** and version 1 stays readable and marked replaced; the bank file is voided if one was downloaded and a fresh download is required; audit; the staff member is told (no figures); refused once the month is Paid or when the preparer rule would be broken per the spec.
3. **The bank file** (Accountant, after publish): bank only; a generic CSV with the columns the spec lists, replaceable per bank behind a small interface; people with no bank account listed and blocking per the spec; casual pay is never in it; downloading is audited and recorded so Mark as paid can require a current download.
4. **Mark as paid and the lock** (Accountant, PIN): requires a current bank-file download; month becomes PAID and **every write is refused afterwards** (a database-level guard as well as the service, so a bug cannot bypass it); the dialog's plain wording is the client's job, the refusal codes and messages are yours. Extend the `payroll_months_approved_has_signature` check and `unpaidMonthKeys`, and re-check that the "cannot switch a clock off while a month is unpaid" rule still works after Paid.
5. **Documents and exports** (Accountant, HR and Director per the spec's table; Branch Managers never): the payroll **register** PDF (landscape, grouped by department, page plan as the spec), the **summary** PDF, the **Excel workbook** with the sheets the spec lists, the **statutory schedules** Excel, and **all payslips** as one file; each built from the frozen snapshot, stamped "Draft, not approved" until approval, numbered PAY-yyyy-mm with the signature and QR once approved; audited per idempotency key; size, rate and time limits as in 1A's exports; never any data from outside the snapshot.
6. **Hand-over to Segment 3:** expose exactly what the payslips spec says it receives (published payslips, versions, the snapshot document, a hook for "a payslip was published or corrected" notifications), so the phone back end needs no changes to payroll tables. Audit the open of someone else's payslip is Segment 3's job: do not build it.
7. **Access:** add the new capabilities to the one access table and log them in `slice-0-contract.md` §12.
**Out:** staff payslip phone endpoints, casual pay recording, the P9 (Segment 3); any frontend; changing legacy tables.
