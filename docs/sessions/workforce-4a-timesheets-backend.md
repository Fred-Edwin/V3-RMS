### What the slice is, in plain words
The rota is the promise and the clock events are the fact (slice 3). The **timesheet is the approved truth** that payroll reads. This slice builds: a period per site with weekly and daily timesheets assembled from the clock events and the rota; **automatic approval of days and weeks that match**, so managers only see exceptions; overtime with its evidence and limits (approving overtime only authorises the time, payroll decides pay); the period closing by itself at the cut-off (a manager may close early; anything still open goes to HR); the landscape timesheet report data (TSH-0007); **Report a problem** (a person says what a recorded time should be), **My requests** (waiting, escalated to HR after 3 days, answered with before and after) and the "clocked out by mistake" request that slice 3 deliberately left out; and the data for **My time** and **My rota** on the phone.

### Safety
Everything is additive. The legacy payslip and attendance code keeps running untouched. Timesheets are built **only from the new attendance events**, so they exist only for sites whose attendance switch is on (off everywhere today; pilot Nyeri Town). A site with the switch off has no timesheets and nothing changes for it. **Do not alter legacy tables or services.** Slice 3's hook `setPeriodClosedCheck` is yours to implement: a closed period refuses fixes (`PERIOD_CLOSED`) except HR's reopen path.
