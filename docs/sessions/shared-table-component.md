## The convention (already decided; do not redesign it)
Read `docs/UI_BUILD_RULES.md` §4 and **§4a, "The one table convention"**. In short:
1. **Search and filters first**: one bar above the header with a search box, status chips (with counts), and filter dropdowns reading "Name · All" at the right (Category, Type, Department, Section and the like, reusing the Catalog's filter names).
2. **Type-ahead search**: results from the first letter, no Enter key; matching letters bold; a line saying what matched ("3 matches for 'oat'"); filters still apply; clearing restores the list.
3. **Pagination, never infinite scroll**: footer "Showing 1–50 of 142" on the left; "Rows per page" selector (25, 50, 100; default 50) and numbered pages (‹ 1 2 3 … 12 ›) on the right; current page dark, unusable arrow grey.
4. **State in the URL** (page, rows per page, search, filters); changing a filter or the search returns to page 1.
5. **Exception**: lists the user drags to reorder (Count setup) show the whole section on one scrollable list with no pager.
Two items in that rule are owner-unconfirmed proposals: the 25/50/100 sizes and URL-held state. Build them as written, keep them easy to change, and report them back in your recap.
